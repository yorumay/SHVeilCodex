Mystical ascetics and holy wanderers who bridge the physical realm and the spiritual world. Unlike the cloistered temple priests or the martial [[sohei]], shukenja achieve their spiritual power through intense physical hardship, mountain isolation, long fasts, and solitary meditation under freezing waterfalls. By attuning themselves to both the [[Great Dharma]] of the [[Way of Enlightenment]] and the elemental forces of the kami, they channel divine miracles - purifying curses, healing the wounded, warding against evil spirits ([[yokai]]), and invoking nature's wrath against sacrilege. Though often detached from worldly politics, their wisdom and spiritual favor are sought by peasant villages and mighty [[daimyo]] alike.
