The standard unit of economic and political wealth, defined as the amount of rice required to feed one adult human for a full year (roughly 180 litres or 330 pounds). A lords's power, army capacity, and court rank are measured directly in domain yield (e.g., a "100,000-koku domain"). Taxes, samurai stipends, and military levies are all calculated in koku, making grain granaries, rice yields, and harvest raids primary strategic targets.
