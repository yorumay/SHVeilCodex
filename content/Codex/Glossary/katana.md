The iconic, gently curved single-edged sword worn by the samurai class. Masterfully folded by skilled blacksmiths to achieve a razor-sharp edge backed by a resilient steel spine, the katana is far more than a battlefield weapon - it is legally recognized as the very symbol of warrior's noble status and personal honor. Paired with a shorter companion blade (wakizashi), carrying the dual blades (daisho) is an exclusive privilege reserved strictly for samurai.
