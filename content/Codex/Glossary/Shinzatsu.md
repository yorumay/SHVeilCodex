An elite adventuring guild composed of six formidable heroes who have risen to prominence across the realm. Prophesied by the heavenly kami themselves as the mortal champions destined to walk in the footsteps of the triune deities - [[Gond]], [[Mystra]], and [[Tyr]] - the Shinzatsu bear the cosmic burden of subduing and binding the nightmare entity [[Kezef]] the Chaos Hound once more before it can devour the cosmos. Currently operating under an official stipend granted by the late shugo-daimyo of [[Shizume]] Province.
