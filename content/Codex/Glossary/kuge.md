The ancient, hereditary nobility who reside in the imperial capital and populate the high offices of the [[Imperial Court]]. Claiming divine descent from the [[kami]] of the [[Age of Gods]], the kuge are masters of courtly ritual, classical poetry, calligraphy, and high diplomacy. Though they often lack the standing armies commanded by provincial [[daimyo]], their immense traditional prestige, marriage alliances, and control over religious appointments grant them quiet, far-reaching power across [[Kozakura]].
