Highly trained professional artists skilled in classical music, traditional dance, poetry, conversation, and the games of the "Floating World" (Ukiyo). Maiko are young apprentices - distinguishable by their elaborate floral hairpins (kanzashi), high wooden clogs (okobo), and brightly colored long-sleeved kimono - training under an established mistress. Neither prostitues nor court courtesans, geisha are refined cultural masters whose private banquet rooms serve as premier neutral ground for high-stakes political intrigue, espionage, and secret negotiations.
