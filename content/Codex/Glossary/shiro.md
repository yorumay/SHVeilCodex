Formidable, multi-tiered fortress complexes engineered from massive, inward-curving stone foundations (ishigaki) and heavily plastered, fire-resistant wooden keeps (tenshu). Built on strategic hilltops or river bends, a shiro is a maze of defensive courtyards, narrow kill-zones, sliding gates, arrow slits (sama), and hidden drop-chutes for dropping heavy stones on attackers. Beyond their military function, these grand keeps serve as the administrative seats and display an absolute power for regional daimyo.
