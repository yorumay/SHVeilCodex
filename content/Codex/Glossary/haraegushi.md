A sacred wooden wand featuring a cluster of zig-zagging white paper streamers (shide) bound to its tip. Used extensively by [[shukenja]], [[miko]], and [[Shinto]] priests during harae purification rituals, the *haraegushi* is rhytmically waved over individuals, sacred relics, or contaminated threshold sites to sweep away the spiritual pollution ([[kegare]]), ward off malevolent spirits, and channel divine blessings.
