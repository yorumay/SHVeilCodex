A curved, single-edged short sword with a blade length between one and two feet. Worn at the waist alongside the longer katana, the wakizashi served as a samurai's close-quarters weapon inside tight corridors, a defensive off-hand blade, and the official instrument used to perform ritual suicide (seppuku). Unlike the katana, which was removed when entering a lord's manor, a samurai kept their wakizashi worn at all times - earning it the reputation of the warrior's "companion blade."
