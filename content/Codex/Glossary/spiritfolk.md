Humans whose bloodlines have been blessed - or altered - by direct contact with kami, elemental spirits, or nature deities prior to their births. Divided into Bamboo, River, and Mountain branches, spiritfolk resemble humans but possess striking otherworldly features: hair like woven pine needles, eyes the color of deep river pools, or an innate immunity to drowning and wilderness hazards. They walk easily between mortal society and the spirit world.
