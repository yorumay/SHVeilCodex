A centuries-old ceremonial and military title bestowed upon the chosen leader of the mountain settlement of Toturi - currently held by the patriarch Ishikui Iwao. Far more than a mere village elder, the *Stonewatcher* bears the solemn, perpetual duty of monitoring the deep mines and abyssal fissures beneath the mountains. It is the Stonewatcher's burder to read the shifting slate, ward the ancient tunnels, and ensure whatever slumbers or lurks in the lightless under-realms never breaks out into the Middle Realm.
