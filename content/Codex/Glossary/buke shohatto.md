The legal edicts issued by the [[bakufu|shogunate]] to regulate the conduct, military strength, and peronal lives of the [[samurai]] class. These laws restrict castle fortifications, ban unauthorized marriages between warlord families, regulate [[yoroi|armor]] and [[daisho|weapons]], and forbid harbored criminals. Violating the Buke Shohatto gives the central government a legal reason to strip a lord of his land ([[kaiki]]) or execute his house.
