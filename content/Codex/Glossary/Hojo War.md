A bloody, six-year civil war that devastated [[Kozakura]] following the premature death of [[Shogun]] Hojo Kikutake. Because both Kikutake's young son and his grandson were under the legal age of majority (eighteen), rival factions within the [[Hojo]] clan clashed violently for control of the regency ([[shikken]]). The realm split along factional lines, culminating in widespread naval engagements across the Inland Sea and devastating land campaigns.

The conflict reached its bloody climax at the historic Battle of [[Norinoshima]], where [[Takenaka Sugawara]] - half-brother to [[Takenaka Okawa|Okawa]] - distinguished himself while commanding the forces in defense of the [[Hojo Kawabuko|grandson]]'s claim. At dawn, just as the lines converged, the senior [[Hojo]] general Nikken Yamashita defected alongside his entire army to Takenaka's side, crushing the opposing alliance. The victory resulted in the banishment of [[Hojo Todahiro|Kikutake's son]], the installation of the [[Hojo Kawabuko|grandson]] as [[shogun]], and the firm establishment of [[Takenaka]] control over the shikken regency.
