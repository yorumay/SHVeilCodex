A sinister secret society composed of reclusive, evil wu jen who have set aside their usual solitary nature to forge a dangerous alliance. Operating out of their hidden headquarters in the grand city of Dojyu, the Society maintains a tight cell structure to preserve absolute secrecy - utilizing esoteric recognition signs, lethal trials for prospective initiates, and swift execution for those who risk exposure. Within their cloistered circles, members share forbidden arcane knowledge, conduct joint magical rituals, and extend mutual protection and resource aid to one another.

The Iridescent Peacock Society prospers amidst political chaos and military strife. To ensure maximum profit, influence, and upheaval, the cabal covertly finances, advices, and supplies magical aid to opposing sides of brewing civil conflicts across Kozakura - fanning the flames of war while quietly harvesting the spoils from the shadows.
