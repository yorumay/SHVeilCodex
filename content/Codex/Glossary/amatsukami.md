The celestial kami originating from [[Takamagahara]] (the High Heavenly Plain). Led by the Sun Goddess [[Amaterasu]], the amatsukami represent order, divine authority, light, and the celestial hierarchy. [[tenno|Imperial]] bloodlines and high noble houses trace their divine right to rule back to these heavenly spirits, who established dominion over the [[nakatsukuni|mortal realm]] in primordial times.
