A system of ritualistic hand seals (kuji-in) and quick diagonal slashing gestures used to channel spiritual energy, focus the mind, and cast protective magic. By weaving specific hand positions while chanting nine sacred syllables - Rin, Pyo, To, Shai, Kai, Jin, Retsu, Zai, Zen - a practitioner cuts a grid in the air to form an invisible barrier.
