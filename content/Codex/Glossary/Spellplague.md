A cataclysmic disaster of cosmic proportions that struck Realmspace, the Prime Material Plane and the outer planes on Tarsakh 29, 1385 DR (The Year of the Blue Fire). Known to the inhabitants of Abeir as the Blue Breath of Change, the true cause of this cosmic tearing remains shrouded in mystery.

The onset of the Spellplague ushered in the dark era known as the Wailing Years, during which arcane magic destablized and completely ceased to function in its traditional forms. Cascading waves of cerulean fire swept across Toril, rending landmasses, fusing planar boundaries with Abeir, and mutating living beings into horrors or spellscarred abominations. While the realm slowly picks up the pieces, ancient prophecies have recently resurfaced across the realms, ominously foretellign the coming of this reality-shattering calamity.
