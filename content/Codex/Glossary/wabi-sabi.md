A core philosophical worldview centered on finding beauty in imperfection, simplicity, and the natural cycle of decay. Wabi refers to the quiet elegance of rustic simplicity, while sabi is the beauty gained through age, wear, and patina. This aesthetic defines everything from moss-covered shrines and cracked tea bowls mended with gold (kintsugi) to aging warrior-poets who value a weathered blade with history over a polished armament.
