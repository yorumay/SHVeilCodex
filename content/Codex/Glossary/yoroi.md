The intricate, heavy armor worn by samurai warriors, designed to maximize protection while allowing mobility for archery and swordsmanship. Constructed from overlapping scales or plates of iron and heavy leather laced together with silk cords (odoshi) and coated in waterproof lacquer, a full suit of yoroi includes a crested helmet (kabuto), a menacing face mask (menpo), chest cuirass (do), shoulder guards (sode) and armored sleeves (kote).
