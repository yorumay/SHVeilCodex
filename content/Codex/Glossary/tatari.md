A potent, lingering curse or affliction brought about by disturbing a sacred site, desecrating a shrine, or invoking the wrath of a powerful spirit ([[kami]] or [[goryu]]). Unlike ordinary spell-curses cast by mortal mages, a tatari represents environmental or cosmic vengeance - manifesting as sudden illness, sudden crop failure, bizarre weather, or madness until proper purification rituals ([[harae]]) and appeasement offerings are performed.
