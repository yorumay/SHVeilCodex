A terrifying, iconic mask depicting a soul consumed by obsessive jealousy, tragic grief, and demonic rage. Featuring sharp metallic horns, wide bulging eyes, and a gaping mouth full of fangs, the mask's expression subtly shifts depending on the light: viewed from above, it appears weeping and heartbroken, but tilted downward, it displays a menacing, murderous sneer. In Noh and Kabuki plays - as well as actual encounters - the hannya represents a mortal woman whose intense heartache has transformed her into a vengeful demon (oni).
