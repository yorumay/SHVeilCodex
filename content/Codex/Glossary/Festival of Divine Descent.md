A grand, week-long nationwide celebration commemorating the mythical ascent and coronation of Emperor Mori, the first ruler of the Akimatsu Dynasty. Blessed by the peak blooming of the cherry blossoms (sakura), the entire realm pauses for seven days of grand feasts, street parades, theatrical performances, and shrine offerings. It is a time when court rivalries and regional feuds are temporarily put on hold, as peasants and nobles alike gather under the falling petals to toast to the eternal favor of the heavens.
