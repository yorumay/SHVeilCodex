Notorious maritime raiders, smugglers, and freebooters who haunt the archipelago's coastal waters, islands, and trade lanes. Composed of rogue [[samurai]], desperate fishermen, outlaws, and foreign buccaneers, *wako* bands operate from hidden island coves and river inlets, preying on merchant junks, raiding defenseless coastal hamlets, and frequently clashing with [[bakufu|shogunal]] naval fleets.
