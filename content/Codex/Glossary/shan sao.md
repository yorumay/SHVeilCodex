A reclusive race of small, stocky humanoids native to the dense forests of [[Kara-Tur]], easily recognized by their slightly pointed ears, broad noses and large feline eyes. Fiercely confident and protective of their privacy, shan sao are exceptionally capable martial combatants despite their small stature. They defend their forest territories with ruthless efficiency - relentlessly pursuing retreating intruders using blow darts, short swords and thrown *uchi-ne* darts. 

In battle and survival, shan sao possess potent innate magic: they enjoy complete immunity to fear, can shapechange into ravens or crows, and can cast arcane spells. Most strikingly, they can magically summon tigers from within half a mile to fight alongside them.

Shan sao live in circular woodland villages of up to 80 inhabitants, building simple bamboo huts surrounding a central communal hearth. Their society exists in a deep symbiotic bond with local tiger prides, who act as both spiritual protectors and absolute political judges: once a year, village elders assemble their local tiger allies to evaluate the ruling chief's competence. If the tigers judge the chief's reing inadequate, they choose a successor - a divine verdict the shan sao accept without question.
