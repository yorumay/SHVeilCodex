The divine spirits, venerated ancestors, and natural forces that inhabit the world. Kami can range from paowerful nature deities governing storms, suns, and mountains to localized spirits residing in ancient trees, rivers, waterfalls, or sacred stones. They are not omnipotent or strictly moral beings, but rather manifestations of spiritual force (tama) that can grant blessings when revered or inflict curses (tatari) when angered or neglected.
