The overarching title for any powerful, high-ranking noble or warlord who commands vast estates, private samurai forces, and significant wealth. While the title spans centuries, in the Sengoku era it specifically refers to autonomous rulers - ranging from noble-born shugo-daimyo to ruthrlessly oppoturnistic sengoku-daimyo who claimed their domain through conquest alone. To be a daimyo is to answer to no higher power unless forced to by blade or treaty.
