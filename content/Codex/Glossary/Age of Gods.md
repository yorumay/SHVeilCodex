The primordial mythical era preceding mortal history, during which the divine creator deities - including [[Izanagi]], [[Izanami]], and [[Amaterasu]] - shaped the heavens, the lands of [[Toril]], and the island archipelago of [[Kozakura]]. In [[shinto|Kozakuran mythology]], this era established the sacred order of the realm, birthed the first spirits ([[kami]]), and laid the divine lineage from which the Imperial Throne claims its unbroken right to rule.
