Fanatical, relentless witch-hunters and militant enforcers in service to [[Cyric]], the Dark Sun and Prince of Lies. Encased from head to toe in towering suits of Gondar Armor, these inquistors operate as unstoppable juggernauts dedicated to rooting out heresy, crushing rival cults, and hunting down all who oppose Cyric's supremacy - reserving particular, obsessive hatred for the worshipers of [[Bane]]. Because their wargear relies entirely on mundane mechanical genius rather than the unstable Weave, Cyric's Inquisitors march fearlessly through spellfire and magical wards to execute their targets.
