The largest class of heavy warships in the [[Kozakura]]n naval fleet, essentially functioning as floating wooden castles. Featuring thick timber hulls reinforced with iron plating, multi-story firing towers for archers and gunners, and massive oarsmen crews, *atakebune* dominate major coastal sieges and ocean blockades. Though slow and unwieldy in shallow coastal waters, their sheer size and defensive bulk make them virtually impenetrable to smaller pirate craft.
