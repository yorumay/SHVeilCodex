A traditional flute carved from the dense root end of a bamboo stalk. Originally played by itinerant Buddhist monks (komuso) of the Fuke sect as a tool for breath-meditation (suzen), the monks wore deep woven reed baskets (tengai) over their heads to detach from ego. Because of their thick, heavy bamboo construction, a shakuhachi doubles as an effective, concealable club - making the basket-wearing komuso a classic disguise for wandering spies, assassins, and undercover samurai.
