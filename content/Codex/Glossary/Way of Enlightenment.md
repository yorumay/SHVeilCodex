The dominant philosophical and spiritual faith of [[Kozakura]], originally brought across the sea by missionaries from [[Shou Lung]] in 462 DR during the reign of [[tenno|Emperor]] Shotoken. The core purpose of the faith is to guide practitioners toward spiritual perfection, ethical living, and ultimate liberation by drawing profound lessons from the lives, doctrines, and speeches of its venerated historical masters. Full-time devotees include ordained priests, cloistered monks, wandering [[shukenja]], and armor-clad [[sohei]] warrior-monks.

For the general populace, the Way of Enlightenment is rarely practiced in isolation; most common folk and [[samurai]] household blend its teachings with the native worship of the [[Shinto|Eight Million Gods]] ([[kami]]), seeking spiritual salvation through the Way while honoring local spirits for health, bountiful harvests, and protection.
