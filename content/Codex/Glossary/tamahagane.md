The priced, high-purity carbon steel smelted using traditional clay furnaces (*tatara*) from black iron sand (*satetsu*) and pine charcoal. Known as "jewel steel," tamahagane is painstakingly selected, folded, and forged by master swordsmiths to create [[Kozakura]]'s finest [[katana]], [[wakizashi]], and [[yoroi|armor plates]] - yielding blades renowned for their razor sharpness, distinct wave-like edge patterns (*hamon*), and resilience in combat.
