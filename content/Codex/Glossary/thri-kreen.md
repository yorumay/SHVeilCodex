Prantis-like insectoid humanoids native to distant, arid deserts across Toril. Virtually unheard of in Kozakura, a thri-kreen in these lands is an extraordinarily rare sight - viewed by locals as bizarre alien spirit or legendary yokai. Possessing four arms, a hard chitinous exoskeleton, and the ability to camouflage instantly, they communicate via clicks and telepathy, driven by an alien hunter's instinct that respects neither lord nor court.
