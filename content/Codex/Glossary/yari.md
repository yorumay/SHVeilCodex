The traditional Japanese spear, featuring a long wooden shaft tipped with a razor-sharp steel blade ranging from simple straight points (sugu-yari) to trident-like cross-blades (jumonji-yari). The undisputed backbone of feudal battlefield tactics, massed formations of yari wielded by peasant infantry (ashigaru) form impassable walls of steel that can impale charging cavalry and keep lethal monsters at a safe distance.
