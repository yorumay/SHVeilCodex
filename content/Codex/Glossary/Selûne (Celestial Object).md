The pale, luminescent moon that shines in the night sky above [[Toril]]. Named after the goddess of light and navigation, Selûne's silver glow drives back the darkness of night, dictates the flow of oceanic tides, and shapes the magical of the realm. In eastern lands like [[Kozakura]], scholars, mystics, and astrologers closely track Selûne's phases to mark seasonal festivals, map agricultural calendars, and calculate auspicious times for rituals, often comparing her serene face to the celestial domain of local moon deities like [[Tsukuyomi]].
