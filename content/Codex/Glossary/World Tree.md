A colossal, sentient, tree-like cosmic entity that formed the structural backbone of divine planar alignment prior to the cataclysmic Spellplague. Serving as a living bridge that connected celestial realms directly to one another, its roots anchored deep within Dwarfhome and Arvandor, its lower branches cradled the Green Fields, the House of the Triad, and Dweomerheart, its towering trunk ran alongside the House of Knowedge, Golden Hills, and Brightwater, while its uppermost boughs reached the starry Gates of the Moon.

While standard planar travel required mortals to cross through the Astral Plane to reach the Outer Planes via the Prime Material nexus, angelic servants and divine emissaries of the good deities utilized the World Tree as a shortcut - climbing its bark as a "celestial ladder" to bypass the Material Plane entirely, an annoyance the sentient timber begrudgingly endured. The great entity was ultimately shattered and destroyed durign the cosmic collapse of the Spellplague in 1385 DR.
