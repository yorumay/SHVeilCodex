The official compensation paid by a daimyo or the shogunate to maintain their loyal samurai retainers. Because samurai are legally forbidden from engaging in trade or farming, they depend entirely on these stipends - paid directly in bales of rice (hyou) or calculated against domain yield (koku). A samurai's stipend dictates their social standing, armor quality, number of retainers they can field, and the size of their household. Losing or having a stipend cut is often the final push that turns a warrior into a ronin.
