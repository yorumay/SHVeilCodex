A ruthless new breed of warlord defined by raw power, pragmatic rule, and military conquest rather than aristocratic lineage or imperial appointments. Born from the chaos of the Warring States era, sengoku-daimyo carved out their domains by overthrowing weaker superiors, absorbing rival lands, and establishing total control over local trade, laws, and vassal clans. They rule by the blade alone - legitimacy means nothing if you cannot defend your borders.
