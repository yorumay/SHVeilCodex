An executive provincial rank within the [[Kozakura]]n feudal hierarchy, referring to the official military deputy appointed by a provincial governor ([[shugo]]) to handle local governance and martial law on the ground. Because high-ranking shugo often reside at court in the capital city of [[Dojyu]] to participate in high-level imperial politics, the shugodai exercises practical administrative control over the province - managing tax collections, directing local garrison samurai, maintaining public order, and enforcing regional decrees.
