A fanatical, bloodthirsty offshoot of the Konjo school of the Way of Enlightenment, operating from an impenetrable mountain fortress high on the slopes of the volcanic Daidake. Banished for their horrific twisted doctrines, the Red Fog has discarded peaceful meditation in favor of elemental fire worship, bloody ascetics, and ritual human sacrifice - offering live victims to the boiling magma vents to appease dark spirits and forge forbidden esoteric power.

Operating as a militant religious rebellion (ikko-ikki), the cult's influence stretches far beyond their volcanic domain. Through terror and dark spiritual bargains, they have subverted local rule - most notably redicing Suzuki Motosune, the shugo-daimyo of Tenmei, to a hollow puppet leader. Cunning, secretive, and deeply entrenched, the Temple of the Red Fog seeks to consume Kozakura in purifying flame, replacing both imperial decree and shogunal law with their own terrifying zealotry.
