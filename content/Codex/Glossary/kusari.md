The intricate, lightweight chainmail mesh constructed from small, interlocking iron rings. Unlike heavy Western mail coats, kusari in Kozakura is typically sewn directly onto fabric or leather backings to reinforce vulnerable joints - such as armored sleeves (kote), neck guards, or thigh protectors - without adding unnecessary weight. It is also used independently by covert agents and bodyguards to construct lightweight, easily concealable under-armor (kusari katatabi) that can stop a hidden dagger or sudden slashing blow beneath formal robes.
