A rare, highly prized variant of the traditional red death-lily, blooming exclusively in late autumn and harvested in the final days before the season's first snow fall. Highly valued by herbalists and apothecaries, the flower possesses potent natural healing and toxin-cleansing properties when brewed into salves and teas. Furthermore, its petals produce a vibrant, pure-white powder dye that has become famous among [[geisha and maiko]] as a prized, safe alternative to traditional lead-based face cosmetics ([[oshiroi]]).
