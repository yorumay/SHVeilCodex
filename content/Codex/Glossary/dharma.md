The fundamental moral order, cosmic law, and righteous duty that governs all existence within the universe. In [[Kozakura]]n society, dharma encompasses both the cosmic balance of nature and an individual's social, moral, and spiritual obligations according to their station - from the peasant tending rice paddies to the [[samurai]] wielding a [[katana]] in service to their lord. Living in harmony with one's dharma cultivates spiritual purity, right action, and positive karma across generations.
