The distant, esoteric pantheistic faith originating from the far-western realms across the Trackless Sea. Focused on a pantheon of olympean deities and subterranean chthonic powers, Hellenism views death and mortal destiny as a guided journey through distinct spiritual gates. Within [[Kozakura]], its traditions are rarely practiced in their pure form; instead, its rites have been quietly absorbed and syncretized by secretive sects - most notably the cultists of the [[Temple of the Red Fog]].

# Hermes
Within the syncretic rites fo the [[Temple of the Red Fog]], the foreign deity Hermes is revered specifically in his chthonic aspect as Psychopompos - the divine guide of departed souls. Devotees pray to the winged herald to seek out souls stranded in the mist, protect wandering spirits from predatory demon-spirits ([[yokai]]), and lead the deceased safely from the physical world toward the thresholds of judgment.

# Hades
Revered by the [[Temple of the Red Fog]] as the absolute judge of the underworld. In their doctrine, Hades sits atop an unyielding throne before the halls of the afterlife, weighing the mortal deeds, oaths, and sins of all who pass before the gods. None may cross into their final eternal resting place without first receiving his severe, impartial verdict.

# Lampades
Underworld nymphs and torch-bearing spirits of the foreign faith. In the liturgy of the Red Fog, the Lampades are invoked during funeral rites and nocturnal vigils as celestial light-bearers. Holding pale, cold-flame torches, these spectral maidens illuminate the pitch-black tunnels and foggy riverbanks of the underworld so that lost souls do not stumble into the abyssal void.

# Hecate
The three-faced foreign goddess of witchery, night, and spiritual threshold. Worshiped by the Temple as the vigilant protector of subterranean crossroads, Hecate wards the winding paths of the dead - ensuring that passing souls are not misled by deceitful [[yokai|phantoms]] or stray from the narrow trail leading toward judgment.

# Hypnos
The gentle god of sleep and tranquil dreams. Within the [[Temple of the Red Fog]]'s funerary traditions, Hypnos is invoked to grant the [[tama]] (soul) a peaceful transition into the afterlife - soothing the terror of death, hushing lingering eartly regrets, and ensuring that the faithful slumber undisturbed by necromantic spells or restless curse-afflictions ([[tatari]]).

# Charon
The grim, silent chthonic boatman who navigates the dark waters separating the living realm from the underworld. Temple rites dictate the living playing the toll for Charon, by placing a coin on his palm, to secure safe passage across the mist-shrouded river to the halls of judgment.

# Lethe
Revered by the [[Temple of the Red Fog]] as the ultimate, merciful goddess of the dead and guardian of the River of Forgetting, whose waters wash away mortal grief and memories before rebirth. However, the Temple's priesthood has committed a tragic, catastrophic error: the divine entity they worship as "Lethe" is, in truth, a terrifying distorted avatar of [[Izanami]] - the vengeful, death-bound Mother Goddess of Yomi-no-Kuni. Unbeknownst to the faithful, every prayer and offering directed to Lethe slowly feeds the dark, corrupting reach of Izanami's underworld domain.
