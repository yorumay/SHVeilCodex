A deep, domed basket-hat woven from reed or straw, traditionally worn by mendicant wandering monks to completely cover the head and face. Featuring small woven slits to see through, the *tengai* symbolizes teh detachment from personal identity and ego. Due to the anonymity it affords, it is also frequently favored by traveling spies, [[ninja|assassins]], and masterless [[ronin]] seeking to travel without drawing attention.
