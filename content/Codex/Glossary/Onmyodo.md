A traditional system of natural philosophy, divination, and esoteric magic practiced by [[onmyoji]] (diviners and court mages). Blending cosmic balance, elemental theory, and spiritual containment, *onmyodo* deals heavily with calculating auspicious dates, reading celestial alignments, warding against malevolent spirits ([[yokai]] and [[mononoke]]), and binding [[shikigami]] paper-servants to carry out a practitioner's will. It is widely patronized by imperial courts, noble houses, and provincial authorities seeking protection from supernatural threats.
