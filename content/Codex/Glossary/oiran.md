High-ranking, aristocratic courtesans of the pleasure districts (yuukaku) who represent the pinnacle of grace, beauty, and refined arts in Kozakuran high society. Far beyond common entertainers, an oiran is a master of music, poetry, calligraphy, tea ceremony, and high-stakes political intrigue. 

Because of their immense cultural prestige, an oiran retains absolute authority over her clientele; even powerful daimyo and wealthy merchants must undergo a lengty, extraordinarily expensive courting ritual - and be deemed worthy by the oiran herself - before securing an audience. Standing outside of the standard feudal hierarchy, their private parlors often serve as neutral ground where regional secrets are traded, political alliances are forged, and hidden power brokers move behind silk screens.
