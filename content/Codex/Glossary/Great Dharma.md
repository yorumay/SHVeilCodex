The complete, overarching spiritual body of divine truth, ultimate reality, and sacred doctrine revealed by the great teachers of the [[Way of Enlightenment]]. While ordinary *[[dharma]]* relates to societal duty and moral law, the Great Dharma represents the ultimate path to spiritual liberation, awakening, and transcendence over suffering and the cycle of rebirth. It is the core doctrine studied by priests, pondered by scholars, and channeled through the ascetic rites of [[shukenja]] across the realm.
