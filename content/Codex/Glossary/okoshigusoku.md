The standardized, mass-produced lacquered chest armor worn by [[ashigaru]] and lower-ranking retainers in military levies across [[Kozakura]]. Consisting of hinged iron or toughened leather plates coated in black or red weather-resistant lacquer, the *okushigusoku* is designed for rapid mass manufacture and ease of maintenance in the field. They are frequently painted with the [[mon]] (heraldric crest) of the ruling clan or [[daimyo]] on the front breastplate for quick battlefield identification.
