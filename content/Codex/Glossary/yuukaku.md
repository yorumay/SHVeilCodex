Designated, walled urban entertainment districts located within major port cities and provincial capitals. Enclosed by moats or high gates and regulated by local authorities, a *yuukaku* houses teahouses, [[kabuki]] theatres, gambling dens, and courtesan manors. While centers of art, fashion, music, and nightlife, they are also dense web-works of political intrigue, underworld dealings, and clandestine meetings between rival factions.
