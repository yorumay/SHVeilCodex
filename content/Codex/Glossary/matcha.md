A vibrant, finely ground powdered green tea cultivated from shade-grown tea leaves, central to Kozakuran hospitality and the formal tea ceremonies ([[chado]]), matcha is prepared by whisking the ground powder into steaming water using a fine bamboo whisk (chasen). It is prized by monks, court nobles, and samurai alike for its earthy flavor, meditative preparating, and invigorating clarity of mind.
