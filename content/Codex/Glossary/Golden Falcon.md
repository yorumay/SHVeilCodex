A wealthy, ambitious adventurer guild operating out of the fortified district of [[Majima]]. Founded, financed, and directed by [[Takatsukasa Hideo]] - the arrogant, deep-pocketed heir to [[Takatsukasa]] clan's vast agricultural fortune - the guild spares no expense in outfitting its mercenaries, spellcasters, and scouts with top-tier gear, fine steel, and lavish bounties.

While the Golden Falcon presents itself as a noble band of heroic protectors safeguarding the [[Tairayama]] foothills, [[Takatsukasa Hideo|Hideo]] uses the organization to expand his family's influence, secure valuable relics, and undercut competing mercenary bands across the region. Armed with the clan's considerable granary wealth and political backing, the guild frequently snatches high-paying imperial contracts and ancient dungeon claims out from under their rivals.
