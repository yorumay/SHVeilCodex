The strictly mandated formal kneeling posture required when sitting on tatami mats in the presence of superiors, during tea ceremonies, or at official court audiences. One sits with knees folded under body, legs flat, and tops of the feet resting on the floor. While the posture demonstrates respect and decorum, remaining in seiza severely restricts quick movement - making a sudden draw of a sword (iaijutsu) from a kneeling position an exceptionally rare, lethal martial art form practiced by elite bodyguards and assassins.
