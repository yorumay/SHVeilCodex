A vital [[Shinto]] ritual of spiritual purification performed by [[shukenja]] and [[miko]] to wash away spiritual pollution ([[kegare]]), evil influences, and misfortune. Utilizing consecrated water, salt, and waving paper-adorned wands ([[haraegushi]]), *harae* is performed before entering holy shrines, prior to [[matsuri|major festivals]], or to cleanse sites tainted by bloodshed, disease, or undead curses.
