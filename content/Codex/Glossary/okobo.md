High, blocky wooden clogs carved from a single piece of light willow wood, traditionally worn by apprentice courtesans ([[geisha and maiko|maiko]]) and high-ranking women of fashion. Distinctive for their hollow slanted from base and tall profile, *okobo* keep expensive, floor-length silk [[kimono]]s lifted safely above muddy streets, unpaved roads, and rain puddles, producing a slow, recognizable bell-like wooden click with every step.
