The licensed residental lodging house where trained courtesans, musicians, and geisha live during their careers and apprenticeships. Managed by a senior matriarch (often called *Oka-san*), the *okiya* pays for the grueling artist training, exquisite silk [[kimono]]s, and living expenses of its residents, directing their bookings and engagements at elite teahouses throughout the local [[yuukaku]] (pleasure district).
