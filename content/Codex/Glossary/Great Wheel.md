The vast, interconnected cosmological structure of the multiverse, encompassing the [[nakatsukuni|Prime Material Plane]], the elemental domains, and the alignment-driven outer planes. In Kozakura, priests, [[wu jen]], and [[shukenja]] view the Great Wheel through an eastern esoteric lens - mapping its outer planes to [[takamagahara|divine spirit realms]], demonic underworlds ([[Codex/Places/Jigoku]]), and elemental balances of wood, fire, earth, metal, and water.
