A wildly popular, highly dramatic form of classical dance-drama known for its stylized performances, elaborate costumes, bold face makeup (kumadori), and grand stage mechanics. Featuring larger-than-life stories of heroic samurai, tragic lovers, and terrifying yokai, kabuki theaters are bustling cultural hubs where commoners, wealthy merchants, and disguised nobles gather - making them ideal venues for secret meetings, public announcements, or subtle political satire.
