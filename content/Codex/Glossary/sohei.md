Monastic holy warriors hailing from powerful, fortified temple complexes built high in the mountains. Armed with traditional heavy naginata polearms and draped in saffron robes over lacquered armor, sohei protect their sacred lands, enforce temple dictates, and play major roles in regional politics. They owe no fealty to no secular daimyo, fighting fiercely to protect their faith's interests or to punish lords who dare infringe on holy soil.
