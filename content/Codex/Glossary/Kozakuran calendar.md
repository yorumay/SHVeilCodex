The official time keeping system of the realm, dating back to Kozakruan Year 1 (-73 DR) with the ascension of the Emperor Akimatsu Mori. Time is tracked in 60-year cycles, written as two numbers separated by a dt or slash: the first representing the era cycle since Emperor Mori's ascension, and the second indicating the specific year within that cycle (for example 25/19  being 1385 DR in the standard Calendar of Harptos). Official imperial decrees, treaty documents, and temple chronicles always record dates in this traditional dual-number format.
