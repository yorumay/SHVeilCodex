Esoteric spellcasters who master magic by studying cosmic interactions of the five elemental forces - Earth, Fire, Metal, Water, and Wood. Unlike court-bound onmyoji, wu jen are solitary hermits, reclusive scholars, and wandering mysteics who live outside standard feudal society. To unlock high arcana, a wujen must abide by strange, binding personal taboos (e.g., never wearing metal, never sitting facing east, or never eating meat), which if broken, strip them of their spellcasting power.
