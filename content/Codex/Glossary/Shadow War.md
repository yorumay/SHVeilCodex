A devastating, clandestine conflict that erupted in 1358 DR and reshaped [[Kozakura]]'s political landscape forever. Historians continue to debate its official end: some claim it concluded with the dawn of the [[Imperial Restoration]], while others mark its finish with the complete extinction of the [[Takenaka]] clan.

The war began abruptly during the autumn festival of [[Highharvestide]], when [[Hojo Kawabuko]] - having barely reached legal age - forged an unprecedented secret alliance with fifteen distinct [[ninja]] clans. Under the cover of night, hundreds of shinobi infiltrated [[Takenaka]] fortresses, garrisons, and noble estates on both sides of the regional conflicts, systematically executing the clan's military and political leadership in a single wave of coordinated strikes. Within three years of unrelenting shadow warfare, the [[Takenaka]] house was declared officially extinct, breaking their grip on power and bringing an abrupt end to the era of the regent [[shogun]]s ([[shikken]]).
