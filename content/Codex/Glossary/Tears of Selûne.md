A dazzling trail of brilliant, star-like asteroids that closely follow the [[Selûne (Celestial Object)|moon]] across the night sky. According to ancient surface legends, these shimmering celestial bodies are tears shed by the [[Selûne (Deity)|Moonmaiden]] herself during her eternal struggle against darkness. Far more than a mere celestial phenomenon, the Tears serve as a crucial aid for sky-watchers, navigators, and astrologers who read their positions to predict tides, divine future omens, and track the shifting currents of cosmic magic across the world.
