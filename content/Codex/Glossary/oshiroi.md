The distinctive, bright white face powder applied by geisha, maiko, kabuki actors, and high court nobles. Traditionally mixed with water into smooth paste and painted onto the face, neck, and chest, oshiroi creates an unblemished, doll-like porcelain aesthetic under warm candlelight. While traditional formulas notoriously contained harmful lead additives, wealthy performers favor safe, luxurious alternatives manufactured from crushed [[shiro higanbana]].
