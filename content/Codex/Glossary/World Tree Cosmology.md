A sweeping cosmological structure first codified in the Year of Wild Magic (1372 DR), rapidly supplanting the classic Great Wheel model as the premier understanding of planar arrangement. The system derives its name from the discovery of a vast, sentient cosmic entity that physically manifests as a brobdingnagian tree (world tree). Roots, trunk, and branches of this divine arbor interface across planar space, bridging disparate realms and allowing planar travelers to navigate between heavens and hells much like mariners utilize the River Styx.

However, the World Tree model is actively being invalidated in the wake of the devastating Night of Bluefire (which some scholars have began to call Spellplague). The catastrophe triggered the collapse of the individual Elemental Planes into the swirling storm of the Elemental Chaos, shattered the Ethereal Plane, and destroyed the World Tree itself - leaving the surviving Outer Planes to drift as isolated astral dominions within the vast expanse of the Astral Sea.
