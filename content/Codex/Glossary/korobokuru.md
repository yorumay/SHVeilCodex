The eastern cousins of Western dwarves, native to the rugged northern wilderness, dense forests, and mountain valleys of Kozakura. Shorter and more hirsute than humans, korobokuru are fierce warriors, master woodcarvers, and deeply spiritual hunters who live in simple subterranean or earthly villages. While wary of human warlords and civilization's expansion, they make loyal allies to those who respect nature and fight alongside them against dark yokai.
