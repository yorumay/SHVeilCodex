A prestigious, highly celebrated theater company based in the port city of [[Fukama]], patronized by provincial governors and visiting court nobles for their mesmerizing, masked [[noh]] drama performances. Beneath their refined artistic facade, however, the troupe maintains a secret pact with the elusive Konishi ninja clan - using their nationwide performance tours, backstage access to noble estates, and costume disguise mastery to gather high-level intelligence and execute clandestine assassinations.
