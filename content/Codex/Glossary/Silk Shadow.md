A clandestine, realm-spanning espionage network composed entirely of courtly women, noble wives, attendants, and estate managers. Centuries ago, the network began informally as noblewomen quietly exchanging gossip, observations, and private secrets about their husbands' political nmaneuverings. Under the sharp, modern leadership of Hisakawa Naomi, this casual salon transformed into a lethal, highly disciplined intelligence organization. Operating beneath the notice of patriarchal warlords, the Silk Shadow gathers secrets, trade logistics, and troop movements from every major faction in Kozakura, channeling a constant stream of vital intelligence into Naomi's private archives.
