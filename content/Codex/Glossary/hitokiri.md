A feared title given to elite, cold-blooded assassins who specialize in state-sanctioned murder or political decapitation. Operating in the shadows, a hitokiri is a master of rapid-draw sword techniques (iaijutsu), stealth, and targeted execution. Ulike high-minded samurai bound by bushido, a hitokiri is a cold weapon of political expediency - used by ambitious lords to erase rivals without launching an open war.
