A foundational pillar of the samurai code ([[bushido]]), meiyo represents pure personal honor, dignity, and moral integrity. Distinct from [[giri and ninjo|giri]] (social duty or obligation) and [[giri and ninjo|ninjo]] (human emotion and compassion), meiyo is an unyielding internal compass - requiring a warrior to judge their own actions honestly, maintain dignity in victory or defeat, and prefer death over a life tarnished by cowardice, deceit, or dishonor.
