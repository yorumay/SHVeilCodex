The formalized martial art and philosophical discipline dedicated to the mastery of swordsmanship. Originating from the ancient battlefield combat styles (*kenjutsu*), kendo emphasizes not merely physical strike accuracy, footwork, and reaction speed, but the absolute unification of mind, body, and spirit (*ki-ken-tai-ichi*). Practice is typically conducted using split bamboo swords (*shinai*) and protective armor (*bogu*), servign as both martial conditioning for samurai and a path toward spiritual refinement.
