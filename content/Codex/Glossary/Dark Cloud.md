A ruthless, deeply entrenched yakuza syndicate operating out of the provincial city of Gifu. Unlike crude street gangs or petty smugglers, the Dark Cloud specializes in high-level blackmail, extortion, and political manipulation - quietly positioning its operatives to pull the strings of provincial military governors (shugo), local magistrates, and powerful samurai houses.

Operating under the surprisingly permissive, watchful eye of the Shogunate - who value the gang as an unofficial tool for keeping regional warlords compromised and divided - the syndicate led by the enigmatic Watagawa Takeshiro. Originally an exile from the rival foreign nation of Wa, Takeshiro's lethal political cunning, vast network of informants, and dossier of noble secrets have made the Dark Cloud one of the most dangerous behind-the-scenes power brokers in Kozakura.
