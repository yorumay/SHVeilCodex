The solemn, ancient coronation rites of the Onmyodo, performed exclusively under the direct blessing of the Imperial Court in Dojyu. Unlike modern arcane study or localized hedge-magic, passing the Imperial Rites requires a practitioner to align their spirit with the celestial court of Takamagahara and swear absolute loyalty to the divine throne. Those initiated through these rites are granted spiritual authority to bind high yokai, manipulate royal earth-nodes, and perform imperial purifications.
