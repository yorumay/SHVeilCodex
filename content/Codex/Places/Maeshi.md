A small, secluded province occupying a rugged northern peninsula on the western coast of [[Shinkoku]]. Cut off from much of the mainland by towering mountain ranges - including the twin-peaked glacial summit of [[Nidoyama]] - Maeshi is a realm of quiet fishing villages, mountain streams, and ancient folklore. Despite its geographic isolation, the province is famous across [[Kozakura]] for two legendary locations: the Imperial-chartered haven of the [[Doi no Fugu]] inn, and the dark, sea-washed island ruins of [[Ito-jo]] Castle.
