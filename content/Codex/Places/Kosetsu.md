## Key Facts
> Location: Eastern Shizume, on the road from Shogitsu
> Garrison: Permanent; standard complement
> Purpose: Eastern approach defence, border inspection for the Shogitsu road
> Status: Active; routine posting - increased vigilance since recent upheavals

Kosetsu guards the eastern border with Shogitsu, the most geographically remote of Shizume's three border fortresses. The road to Shogitsu winds through forested hills before opening onto the plains of the eastern provinces, and Kosetsu sits where the hills end - a stone gate between two very different landscapes.

The eastern border has historically been the quietest of Shizume's three fronts. Shogitsu is not a militarized province, and the road through Kosetsu carries goods and travelers rather than armies. The fortress maintains its garrison and its walls out of institutional habit as much as strategic necessity.
