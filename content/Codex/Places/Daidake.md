The most volatile and active volcano in all of [[Kozakura]], spewing ash and minor eruptions multiple times daily. Rising ominously over the landscape, the mountain is dreaded across the provinces as the sinister sanctuary of the [[Temple of the Red Fog]] - an exiled, bloodthirsty offshoot of the Konjo School of the [[Way of Enlightenment]]. Operating from their mountain fortress among the sulfur vents, these dark zealots practice human sacrifice and fire worship, extending a terrifying grip of influence over the surrounding lands and puppet rulers like [[Suzuki Motosune]].
