One of the few permanently snow-capped mountains in [[Kozakura]], Nidoyama is a majestetic double peak whose narrow cleft valleys harbor ancient, slow-moving glaciers. Though appearing quiet to passing travelers, the mountain holds deep spiritual significance: its high valleys are home to a steadfast community of [[korobokuru]] who have refused for centuries to abandon their ancestral home. The glacial runoff feeds a network of pristine alpine streams, attracting a large population of river [[spiritfolk]] who maintain sacred bonds with the mountain's waters.
