A historically famous river that once flowed directly through the heart of the ancient provincial capital of [[Fukama]]. Following the catastrophic impact of the [[Bluefire]], the terrain shattered and river was violently split into two distinct branches. As Fukama's outer wards were dragged down into submerged ruins, the Sakuragawa's diverted currents re-routed around the city's perimeter, enveloping the surviving central districts and transforming the ancient stronghold into an isolated island city. Today, navigating its turbulent, mist-shrouded waters requires crossing heavily guarded temporary-bridges or utilizing local ferrymen who know the unpredictable currents left in the Spellplague's wake.
