The formidable ancestral seat of power for the Hojo clan, built as an imposing monument to their centuries of tyrannical dominance over Kozakura. From its impenetrable battlements, the Hojo ruled the empire with an unyielding iron fist - until the catastrophic climax of the Hojo War.

In 1344 DR, a grand coalition of rival noble houses, spearheaded by the rising Takenaka clan, marched across Shinkoku and laid siege to the fortress. After a brutal, bloody assault, the castle fell, culminating in the public execution and mass ritual suicide (seppuku) of the Hojo high command. This catastrophic defeat shattered the clan's supreme grip on the realm and inaugurated the short-lived Takenaka regency - a dark era that only came to a bloody close in 1357 DR when the surviving heir, Hojo Kawabuko, reclaimed the citadel and systematically eradicated the Takenaka during the Shadow War.
