A terrifyin, sunless swamp whose very name strikes dread into the hearts of nearby villagers. Dark, brackish waters cover ancient battlefields and forgotten burial sites, giving rise to restless spirits, hungry [[gaki]] (ghouls), and water-bound undead. Black mud pits and toxic fumes render the terrain nearly impassable without a skilled guide or spiritual protection. Local folklore warns that those who perish within Shibinuma rarely remain at peace, as the miasma rising from the black peat reanimates corpses to wander the endless, reed-choked dark.
