A rugged, independent island nation situated across the stormy northern seas from the mainland [[Kozakura]]. Populated by fiercely self-reliant clans, seafarers, and northern traders, Ryuten maintains a delicate political neutrality toward the [[bakufu|Shogunate]] and [[Imperial Court]]. The island relies heavily on smuggled raw metals and timber - frequently trading with the korobokuru of [[Gosuki]] - in exchange for rare northern furs, sea ivory, and exotic sea-game. Because it falls outside direct imperial and [[bakufu|shogunal]] control, Ryuten has long been a haven for political exiles, masterless [[ronin]], and daring maritime merchants.
