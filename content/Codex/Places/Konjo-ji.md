The priemier monastery of the Konjo (Zen) school of the [[Way of Enlightenment]] in the capital city of [[Dojyu]]. Overseen by the High Pirest Niduro, Konjo-ji champions strict physical discipline, martial training, direct intuition, and austere silent meditation. Highly favored by [[samurai]] houses, commanders, and [[sohei]] warrior-monks, Niduro's teachings stress inner focus and unwavering resolve in the face of death. However, the temple's reputation remains quietly shadowed by its historic connection to the [[Temple of the Red Fog]] - a fanatical, bloodthirsty offshoot that fractured from the Konjo school generations ago.
