The magnificent eastern port of the imperial capital of [[Dojyu]], crafted from heavy cypress beamwork and adorned with gold-leaf imperial chrysanthemum crests. Facing the ocean and the eastern provinces, the Sunrise Gate traditionally symbolizes rebirth, auspicious beginnings, and civic order. It serves as the primary ceremonial entrance for visiting [[shugo-daimyo]], foreign ambassadors, and imperial processions entering the capital at dawn. Because of its grand diplomatic status, the gate is heavily guarded by elite imperial [[samurai]] and flanked by administrative customs offices that inspect all incoming merchant wealth.
