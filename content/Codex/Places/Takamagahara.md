The radiant, divine realm birthed from the very frst light that broke free from the cosmic egg at the dawn of creation. Resting in the highest reaches of the cosmos above the mortal plane, Takamagahara is the ancestral home and golden court of the celestial kami ([[amatsukami]]), ruled over by the Sun Goddess Amaterasu. It is a realm of absolute order, blinding brilliance, and divine law - connected to the mortal world below by the Floating Bridge of Heaven ([[Ame-no-Ukihashi]]). It stands as the ultimate bastion of celestial light against the creeping darkness of [[The Void]] and the shadow-plagued depths of [[Yomi-no-Kuni]].
