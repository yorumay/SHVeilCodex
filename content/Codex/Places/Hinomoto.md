One of the four main islands of [[Kozakura]], revered as the retirement sanctuary of the Heavenly Brother, [[Izanagi]], one of the realm's creator deities. Unlike its mountainous neighbors, Hinomoto lacks high ranges, defined instead by the low, forested ridges of Fokobi Hills and the sprawling Fokobi Forest in Gumi Province. Most islanders make their living through fishing or logging timber for ship repairs. Along its northwest coast dwell tribes of hai nu who guard an underwater gateway to the domain of the Lord of the Sea, while the uninhabited, reef-encircled islet of Itzujima serves as a sanctuary for sea turtles and nesting seabirds.

Dotted from its coasts to deep forest glades with shrines dedicated to [[Izanagi]], Hinomoto is steeped in divine sanctity. By ancient decree of the first [[Tenno|Emperor]], all weapons are strictly forbidden on the island to preserve its holy peace. Though local brigands and desperate travelers sometimes flout the law, carrying open arms is punishable by immediate death, and only [[samurai]] granted special dispensation by the [[shugodai]] may wear their blades openly in settlements.
