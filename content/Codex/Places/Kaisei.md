A wealthy coastal city nestled against the slopes of sacred Seiryuuyama, world-renowned for its lucrative pearl harvesting and deep opal mines. Through spared total annihilation during the Spellplague, a third of the city was consumed by Bluefire - mutating its surviving residents into aggressive, deformed horrors who are now incarcerated within the mountain-carved Kaisei Prison. The city's imposing stone fortress remains held by Shogunate forces, while the city itself serves as a heavily militarized staging ground to maintain an uneasy siege over the castle.
