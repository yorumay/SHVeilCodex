The primary island of Kozakura, stretching over 1,400 miles from tip to tip and serving as the cradle of Kozakuran culture, power, and civilization. Its geography varies wildly - ranging from a sparwling 350 miles across at its broadest to a narrow 70-mile choke point at Miyama Province - and is bisected along its entire spine by the towering Dragon's Spine mountain range. The northern reaches endure freezing, snowbound winters amidst vast pine forests, while the southern lands melt into sweltering, humid bamboo rainforests.

Shinkoku boasts the largest population and the most fertile rice-producing river valleys in the realm, making it the supreme engine of trade and the blood-soaked chessboard of provincial warlords. Coastal fishing hamlets and sprawling agrarian villages dot its fringes, alongside major strongholds such as [[Fukama]] and [[Senita]]. Yet beyond the walls of human settlements lie vast, untamed wildernesses where ancient non-human creatures and enigmatic entities dwell. Indifferent to the shifting tides of samurai politics, these wild denizens rule the mountains - sometimes proving to be brutal monsters, and other times unexpected, fickle allies.
