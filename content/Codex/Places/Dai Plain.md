Sprawling out along the banks of the mighty Dai River, the Dai Plain is the single largest expanse of arable, fertile land in Kozakura. Its endless, water-rich paddies generate the largest rice harvests in the empire, sustaining the dense urban populations of Dojyu and the surrounding provinces. Control over the Dai Plain equates to control over the realm's primary food supply, making its borders some of the most heavily fortified and vigilantly guarded lands in the entire country.
