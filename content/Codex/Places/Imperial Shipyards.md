The premier naval facility and industrial heart of Shogunal maritime power, sprawling across the deepwater harbor of Renkyu. Commissioned to maintain Kozakura's dominance over the Inland Sea and regional coastal waters, the shipyards feature massive drydocks, timber yards stocked with seasoned cypress and cedar, and specialized ironworks. It is here that the realm's largest atakebune floating fortresses and swift patron junks are laid down and fitted for war.

Operated under the direct oversight of Shogunal naval commissioners, the shipyards employ thousands of master shipwrights, caulkers, and blacksmiths. Due to its strategic importance in supplying the Shogunate Navy against wako pirate fleets and rival regional warlords, teh entire harbor district is heavily fortified, patrolled by elite garrison samurai, and guarded against foreign espionage and sabotage.
