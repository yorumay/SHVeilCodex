The largest, longest, and most economically vital river system in all of [[Kozakura]]. Cascading down from the central mountain ranges, the Dai River flows into the sea while nourishing the vast agricultural expanses of the [[Dai Plain]]. It is the lifeblood of the realm - the primary trade artery for merchant barges and the fundamental reason Empress [[Jumei]] chose to found the grand imperial capital of [[Dojyu]] directly along its fertile banks.
