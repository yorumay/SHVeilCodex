A vast, ancient woodland stretching across the coastal reaches of Dosaki Province at the mouth of the Tano River, immediately adjacent to the port city of Renkyu. The forest is renowned throughout Kozakura as the ancestral home of the realm's largest population of bamboo spiritfolk, whose communities live in deep harmony with its ancient groves.

Despite its spiritual significance, the Waturi Forest is also Kozakura's most vital resource for high-grade naval timber. Its massive, straight-grained trees supply the nearby Imperial Shipyards, making it the primary engine for constructing the realm's war fleets. This ongoing timber exploitation is strictly regulated and overseen by the shugo-daimyo of Dosaki, Katsuhara Omitsu - herself a bamboo spiritfolk - who walks a dangerous tightrope between fulfilling the Shogunate's endless demand for warships and protecting her people's sacred homeland from total deforestation.
