A major temple complex situated in the imperial capital of [[Dojyu]], serving as the seat of power for the [[Toro-dai]] (Jodo-shu / Pure Land) tradition of the [[Way of Enlightenment]]. Led by High Priest Kajume, Umoro-ji emphasizes devotion, salvation through faith, and accessible spiritual practice for commoners and nobles alike. Under Kajume's gentle yet shrewd guidance, the temple has cultivated immense popularity among [[Dojyu]]'s populace, drawing massive crowds for public chants and funeral rites while maintaining a diplomatic balance with both the imperial courts and military authorities.
