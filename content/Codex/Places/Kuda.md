The historical capital of Miyama Province under the old Imperial Court's reign. When the Shogun stripped Kuda of its capital status and relocated the provincial government to nearby Tamanokuni, Kuda's economic lifeblood dried up. Today, Kuda stands as a melancholy ghost of its former self - it's noble manors falling into disrepair, its streets sparsely populated, and its remaining inhabitants quietly harboring deep resentment toward the Shogunate for abandoning their ancient, courtly heritage.
