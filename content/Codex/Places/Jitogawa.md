A bustling port city situated on a humid river delta adjacent to the waters of [[Shibinuma]]. As undisputed center of [[Kozakura]]'s silk production, Jitogawa holds a rare imperial dispensation to engage in foreign trade, making its developed harbor a key international gateway - second in commercial output only to [[Renkyu]]. Due to its open maritime ties, Jitogawa hosts more foreign traders than any other city in the realm, though outlanders are strictly restricted in residecne and business to the Foreign Ward nearest the river marshes. During the muggy summer months, the surrounding delta breeds severe outbreaks of malaria and cholera.

The city's daily affairs and commerce are ruled with an iron grip by the [[Silk Za]] - the powerful merchant guild that maintains a rigid monopoly over the domain's entire silk trade, lining the pockets of the shugo-daimyo of [[Naredo]], [[Hojo Tsuneyo]], with lavish kickbacks. Outside the guildhall, Jitogawa serves as [[Kozakura]]'s premier underworld market for contacting black-market smugglers and arranging long-distance clandestine shipping.
