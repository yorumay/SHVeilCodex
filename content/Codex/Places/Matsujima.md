An island situated northwest of [[Gizan]] Province, instantly recognizable by its towering, sheer coastal cliffs and a distinct north-sloping profile. The deep waters separating Matsujima from the Gizan coastline form the deepest and safest navigable channel at the southern mouth of the [[Inland Sea]], making the island of indespensable landmark for merchant captains, naval captains, and sea pilots.

While the island is inhabited by a handful of quiet fishing hamlets, its most guarded secret is that it serves as the secluded refuge of the formidable [[wu jen]] Yosuki. The local villagers tend to her isolated estate, providing food and total secrecy to outsiders regarding her presence. In return for their loyalty, Yosuki uses her high arcane arts to ward of [[wako]] pirate raids and tame the fierce ocean storms that threaten the island's shores.
