A rugged, densely forested province covered in vast, untameable woodlands that have long made it a breeding ground for outlaw syndicates, rogue sects, and anti-[[bakufu|Shogunate]] insurgents. Sogai's deep wilderness conceals the ash-covered ruins of the [[Kurogawa]] clan's ancestral villages, burned to the ground following the Tennu War in a brutal effort to eradicate the region's ninja strongholds.

Today, the province's thick canopy makes official law enforcement nearly impossible, allowing bandit networks to flourish along its travel corridors. The most notorious figure in Sogai's history is Higemoto, a ruthless [[ronin]] turned bandit chief who assembled a force of over one hundred brigands to ambush wealthy merchant caravans and traveling imperial dignitaries along the post road. Far more dangerous than ordinary highwaymen, Higemoto's syndicate operates with tactical precision, actively supported by Katohara, a rogue [[wu jen]], and Nogori, a corrupt monk who uses his religious standing to cloak the syndicate's movements.
