The shimmering, unpredictable twilight realm overlapping the Nakatsukuni, governed by timeless archfey, nature spirit, and noble yosei. A land of eternal autumns, vibrant cherry blossomg groves, and deceptive illusions, the Fey Courts operate on ancient rules of etiquette, debt, and hospitality. Though rarely involved in the petty political squabbles of mortal daimyo, the Courts guard their sacred glens fiercely, making bargains with mortals that often carry unforeseen, lifelong costs.
