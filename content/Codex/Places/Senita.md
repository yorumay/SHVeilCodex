The northern realm's wealthiest city and a towering monument to royal division. Senita is infamous as the "False Capital," having served as teh seat of the rebel Northern Court for 68 years during the Zakura Insurrection (581-648 DR), where a succession of rogue pretenders ruled an independent northern domain.

Thriving on the massive agricultural surplus of the surrounding [[Jodo Plain]], a bustling deep-water seaport, and direct access to the Imperial Post Road, Senita developed into an economic powerhouse that rivals the true capital. Beyond its historical infamy and trade influence, the vast plains encircling Senita are renowned across Kozakura for breeding elite warhorses, making its grand regional horse markets the premier destination for daimyo seeking to outfit their cavalry.
