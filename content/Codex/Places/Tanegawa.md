The largest [[korobokuru]] settlement in [[Tenmei]], located roughly halfway between the [[Gosuki]] and [[Kanfu]] mountain ranges. Tucked away within a sheltered basin, Tanegawa is surrounde on nearly all sides by dense, ancient forests, making it a natural fortress and a sacred haven for its stout, wilderness-dwelling inhabitants.

Historically the premier port and trading hub of [[Tenmei]] - serving as the main conduit for exporting mountain metals and crafted goods across the sea to [[Shinkoku]] - Tanegawa's economic dominance has recently been eclipsed by the rapid rise of [[Yoshida]]. As the [[Temple of the Red Fog]] sparked an explosion of trade and wealth in [[Yoshida]], Tanegawa lost its status as the region's primary port, leaving its traditionalist [[korobokuru]] leaders wary and increasingly suspicious of the growing fanaticism spreading from [[Daidake]].
