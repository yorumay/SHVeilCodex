A rugged coastal province commanding the narrow, treacherous strait between the mainland and the towering cliffs of [[Matsujima]] Island. This deep-water passage represents the safest and most vital naval route at the southern mouth of the [[Inland Sea]]. While Matsujima itself is sparsely inhabited by humble fishing villages, it harbors a dangerous secret: the hidden sanctuary of Yosaki, a powerful [[wu jen]] who protects the islanders from typhoons and [[wako]] pirates in exchange for their absolute silence.

Gizan bears deep scars from the [[Hojo War]]. Once ruled by the Korimori clan (a lineage branch of the Hojo), the province was overwhelmed in 1344 DR when [[Takenaka]] forces shattered their defenses. Today, Gizan is governed by Mashikuni Kakuji, a bitter, disgraced nobleman whose house fell from power following the [[Takenaka]] takeover. Fuelled by old grudges and strategic isolation, Kakuji's court has quietly transformed Gizan into a fertile breeding groudn for anti-[[bakufu|Shogunate]] conspiracies and rebel factions.
