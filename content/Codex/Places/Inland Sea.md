The strategic expanse of ocean nestled between the major landmasses of Shinkoku and Tenmei. Dotted with dozens of inhabited islands, hidden rocky coves, and treacherous channels, these sheltered waters serve as Kozakura's primary commercial artery. Because of its dense trade traffic, the sea is heavily contested battleground - pitted in perpetual naval warfare between the heavily armored Shogunate atakebune war galleons and shift wako pirate fleets operating from hidden island hideouts.

Beyond maritime piracy, the Inland Sea carries a grim history of internal conflict, having served as the primary theatre for decisive naval engagements, troop landings, and coastal sieges during the catastrophic Hojo War. The sea floor remains littered with sunken hills of war vessels, iron armor, and restless aquatic spirits born from centuries of bloodshed.
