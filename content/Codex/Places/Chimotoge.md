A fortified mountain settlement perched directly at the highest peak of Chimo Pass. Wrapped in dense bamboo thickets, sprawling fern beds, and towering cedar forests, Chimotoge serves as a key waypoint, toll station, and garrison town. Once a prideful stronghold of the now-fallen Takenaka clan, the settlement is now firmly occupied by Shogunate-aligned forces under Lord Hojo Katanobu, who keeps a tight iron grip over every traveler, merchant, and messenger ascending the pass.
