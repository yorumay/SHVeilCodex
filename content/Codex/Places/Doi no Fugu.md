A world-renowned inn located at a crossroads in [[Maeshi]] Province, owned and operated by the Sumitomo family. The inn owes its existence and legendary status to an event in [[Kozakuran Calendar|Kozakuran Year]] 1 (-73 DR) following the Battle of Kyoki. The newly ascended [[tenno|Emperor]] [[Akimatsu]] [[Mori]] stayed at the Nami no Hana inn, where a vengeful master chef served him a celebratory dish of raw tiger fugu - sculpted like a flying crane - poisoned with toxic liver. An observent apprentice chef, Sumitomo Tomy, spotted the danger and saved the [[tenno|Emperor]]'s life. When Tomy humbly refused a noble title to honor his family's culinary heritage, the Emperor later surprised him upon completing his training by building a grand, tax-exempt inn at a double-road crossing in [[Maeshi]] and granting Tomy full ownership.

Protected by absolute Imperial decree, any who harm a Sumitomo or violate the inn's hospitality face an immediate death sentence. Passed down through generations from father to eldest son, the inn remains a sanctuary where even deadly enemies must set aside their blades, immortalized in the famous [[noh]] play, "[[The Reward of Tomy]]."
