A historic stone inn situated in the northern province of [[Maeshi]], famously owned and operated by the descendants of the [[Sumitomo]] family for centuries. Gifted to the apprentice chef [[The Reward of Tomy|Sumitomo Tomy]] after he saved the life of [[Tenno|Emperor]] [[Mori]] from fugu poison in ancient times, the establishment enjoys a sacred, perpetual Imperial Decree of Protection. Recognized across [[Kozakura]] as inviolable neutral ground and absolute sanctuary, even the most ruthless [[daimyo]] warlords, and [[ninja|shadow assassins]] dare not draw blood or strike within its walls.
