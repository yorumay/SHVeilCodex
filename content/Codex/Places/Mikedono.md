One of the four major islands comprising the archipelago of [[Kozakura]], situated south of the main island of [[Shinkoku]] at the terminus of the [[Senshi Islands|Senshi Island]] chain. Spanning roughly 200 miles across - making it comparable in size to [[Tenmei]] and [[Hinomoto]] - Mikedono features classic volcanic geography, defined by steep, cloud-piercing central mountains that slope gracefully into wide, fertile coastal plains, pristine beaches, and dense ancient timberlands.

Beyond its natural beauty, Mikedono holds immense economic and martial prestige within the realm: it houses one of [[Kozakura]]'s official imperial mints, striking much of the domain's coinage, and serves as the home of the renowned Light-Sparkling-Water School ([[Mizukirameki-ryu]]), a famed kensai academy dedicated to fluid, lightning-fast sword techniques.
