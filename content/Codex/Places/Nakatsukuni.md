The physical world inhabited by mortals, sprawling cities, and wild wilderness. Positioned directly between the blinding brilliance of the heavenly High Plain ([[Takamagahara]]) above and the cold, shadowy underworld ([[Yomi-no-Kuni]]) below, the [[nakatsukuni|Middle Realm]] is a fragile nexus of physical law and spiritual power. It is the earthly stage where [[kami]], [[yokai]], and mortals coexist, and where cosmic entities from [[Far Realm|The Void]] attempt to bleed into physical reality.
