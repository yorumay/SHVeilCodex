The central name sake of the [[Senshi Islands]] archipelago, Senshi is most densely overgrown island in the chain, thick with tangled vegetation, ancient trees, and hidden spring basins. Nestled along its storm-battered eastward shore stands one of the oldest sea god shrines in all of [[Kozakura]]. Guarded by ancient stone torii gates and washed by the tides, this holy site remains crucial place of pilgrimage for sailors, sea [[spiritfolk]], and [[shukenja]] seeking blessings before daring the unpredictable open ocean.
