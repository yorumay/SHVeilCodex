The massive, continuous mountian range running the entire length of the main island of [[Shinkoku]]. Myth plays that during the ancient Age of Gods, a legendary Earth Dragon was so enchanted by the nascent beauty of the realm that it lay down to rest across the evolving land. Its scaled back hardened into the towering, jagged peaks that dominate Kozakura's landscape today. Local folk believe that the frequent earthquakes shaking the provinces are merely the Earth Dragon stirring fitfully in its deep, restless slumber. The formidable range serves as a daunting natural barrier, with the low, winding [[Chimo Pass]] serving as its primary overland crossing.
