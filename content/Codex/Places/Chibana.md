A pivotal central province whose geographic position makes it one of [[Kozakura]]'s most crucial trade hubs. Its provincial capital, [[Iyabi]], sits directly at a major junction of the [[Imperial Post Road]], where the ancient highway branches nortward as the vital [[Renkyu]] Road. Publicly ruled from [[Iwatenabe]] Fortress by the [[shugo-daimyo]] [[Hisakawa Haruto]], Chibana's strategic roadways and bustling merchant corridors make it a prized prize for military logistics and covert intelligence networks alike.
