Also known as the **Celestial Bureaucracy**, and the **Divine Hierarchy**.

The dominant pantheon and spiritual state governing Shou Lung and its surrounding lands, organized into a strict, cosmic administrative system. The cosmic order is structured from the top down:

- **The Supreme Ruler**: The Celestial emperor.
- **Nine Immortals**: Commanded directly by the Celestial Emperor.
- **Lesser Immortals**: Spirits of holy deceased sages organized into six specialized orders:
	- **Moon Women**: Tenders of heaven who refill the sun's oil lamps, polish the star orbs, and guide the moon and constellations.
	- **Spirit Warriors**: Heavenly champions tasked with instilling the courage and resolve in mortal hearts.
	- **Jade Ladies**: Divine artisans who create precious metals and gems, hiding them deep within the earth.
	- **Rice Spirits**: Stewards governing mortal harvests and soil fertility.
	- **Ladies of Compassion**: Wandering healers roaming heaven and the mortal world (Red Earth) to alleviate suffering.
	- **Lords of Karma**: Heavenly Magistrates who act as defense or prosecution for deceased souls standing before the Lawgiver.
- **The Million Officials**: Minor divine bureaucrats
- **Ancestors**
- **Sages**: Mortal sages who lived a holy life
- **The Others**: Entities standing entirely outside the official bureaucracy - most famously The Monkey, a mischievous, unpredictable demigod who flouts divien protocol at every turn.
