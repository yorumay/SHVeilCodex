A formidable mountain fortress nestled amidst the lush, sub-tropical hills of [[Iwari]] Province, near the bustling city of [[Renkyu]] and commands the surrounding trade routes and river ways. Governed by the imperial loyalist [[Ashigi Desugaki]], this heavily fortified stronghold serves as the principal strategic bastion of the [[Imperial Restoration]] in the south, actively disrupting [[bakufu|Shogunate]] influence and protecting imperial interests across [[Iwari]].
