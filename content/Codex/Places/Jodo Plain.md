Sprawling across the northern expanses of Shinkoku, the Jodo Plain is the second-largest agricultural and rice-producing region in Kozakura. It's fertile, river-fed soil yields the massive food surpluses required to feed the dense populations and armies of the northern provinces. Beyond its vital paddies, the open, rolling pastures of the plain are renowned across the realm for breeding Kozakura's finest warhorses. Whoever controls the Jodo Plain holds a tight grip over both the northern grain supply and the premier cavalry markets of the land.
