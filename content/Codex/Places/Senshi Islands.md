A winding chain of islands varying in size that stretches from the southern isle of Mikedono into the waters of the Gulf of Mi. While some islets are bare, tide-washed rocks, most support lush greenery and sheltered inlets ideal for mooring fishing craft. Eleven major islands anchor the chain: Heike, [[Senshi]], Ojo, Uemi, Shimo, Mishi, Kamine, Tsune, Mifura and Tojuki.

The surrounding waters host some of [[Kozakura]]'s richest fishing grounds, renowned across the empire for extensive oyster beds and lustrous pearls - a trade heavily driven by native sea [[spiritfolk]] pearl divers. While local fishing boats navigate the inner passage, larger junks give the eastern side of the archipelago a wide berth to avoid its treacherous, ship-crushing ocean currents. Among the chain, Kamine and Tsune are barren, waterless crags, whereas the remaining islands are nourished by freshwater springs and natural rock catch-basins.
