A jagged, high-altitude mountain range towering over northern [[Tenmei]]. Its extreme altitudes, relentless blizzards, and unforgiving terrain make the peaks virtually uninhabitable for humans - leaving the region entirely under the stewardship of the [[korobokuru]].

Deep within these frozen heights, [[korobokuru]] miners extract rich deposits of copper, tin, and precious silver veins. The raw ore and refined metals are transported down the mountain trails to the bustling [[korobokuru]] port city of [[Tanegawa]]. 

A significant portion of the metals mined in Gosuki is actively smuggled across the northern straits to the nearby independent island of [[Ryuten]], where local craftsmen rely heavily on [[Kozakura]]n copper and tin for their own metallurgy.
