A once-revered sanctuary built atop Izunayama to honor Izuna's star prophecies. Following the mountain's catastrophic eruption and the tragic, accidental freezing of Fuyukimi, the ashamed monks erased the yuki-onna from their historical record, gradually warping their original doctrine. In recent decades, under the corruption of Abbot Gen'un, the monastery succumbed to heresy - using unknown bio-magical engineering to turn Akujin's Obsidian Heart into a subterranean heating system while worshipping the fire elemental as their reborn god. With the destruction of the Heart and the death of its cult, the monastery stands quiet, its dark secrets exposed to hte sky.
