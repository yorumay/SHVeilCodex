A smaller island sitauted off the southeastern tip of [[Kanshu]], known primarily for its modest tin mines operated by native tribes of [[korobokuru]]. The island's miners trade raw tin and metalwork with [[Iewara]] as well as passing merchant junks. A few years ago, a catastrophic infestation struck Tanekashima when a monstrous insectoid gargantua rampaged across the isle. Though the beast was eventually slain, locals live in lingering terror that its dormant eggs lie hidden in the island's remote hills, waiting for an extended heat wave to hatch and unleash a new plague upon the region.
