A vast, ancient empire occupying much of the kontinent of [[Kara-Tur]], far to the east of [[Faerûn]]. Meaning "Receiver of the Dragon," the empire's founding faith holds that the [[Celestial Emperor|Celestial One]] sent the Nine Great Dragons - the [[Nine Immortals]] - into the lands of mortals to sustain the physical world and govern reality through their pure thought. Drawing inspiration from imperial China, Shou Lung is a land of sprawling cities, vast trade networks, deep court intrigue, and an all-encompassing spiritual bureaucracy that binds the mortal realm to the heavens.
