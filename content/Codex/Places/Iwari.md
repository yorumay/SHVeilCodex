A prosperous, sub-tropical province on the island of shinkoku whose economy is heavily enriched by the mineral-rich mountains lining its borders. The province's bustling trade towns thrive on the mining, cutting, and export of precious stones - most notably high-grade amber, shimering topaz, and rare opals. Governed from the formidable [[Augiyama Castle]] by the imperial loyalist [[Ashigi Desugaki]], Iwari serves as a vital strategic stronghold for the [[Imperial Restoration]].

Despite its wealth, Iwari harbors a notorious geographic hazard: the [[Kebinuma]], a vast, fog-shrouded wetland stretching across its borders. Plagued by localized monster threats and treacherous terrain, regular samurai patrols actively avoid the swamp, turning the [[Kebinuma]] into an ideal staging ground for outlaw bands, rogue [[ninja]] clans, and smuggling rings. The province also bears deep historical scars from the [[Shadow War]]; in 1358 DR, the young commander [[Genzo Taira]] led a devastating raid into Iwari against a hidden stronghold of [[Takenaka]] survivors, burning the castle to ash and cementing the [[bakufu|Shogunate]]'s victory in the region.
