The central heartland of the [[bakufu|Shogunate]] power, dominated by sprawling terraced fields, rich noble estates, and heavily fortified garrisons. The province's defining feature is [[Gifu]], a massive, impenetrable urban center that has served as the official military capital ([[bakufu]]) of [[Kozakura]] since 1046 DR. 

[[Gifu]] was specifically selected by [[Hojo Tademashi]] following the [[Tennu War]] because it sat directly at the geographic and economic nexus of the [[Hojo]] family's vast ancestral estates. From this imposing citadel, the [[bakufu|Shogunate]] projects its military, judicial, and administrative power across all [[Kozakura]], enforcing shogunal decrees, stationing elite [[samurai]] garrisons, and housing the underworld networks like the [[Dark Cloud]] gang and the secret agents of the [[Uji]] clan.
