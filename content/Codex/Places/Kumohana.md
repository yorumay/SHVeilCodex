An isolated agricultural village once vamous across the realm as the sole producer of white spider lilies and their prized non-toxic cosmetic dye. Lacking fertile rice fields, the village relied entirely on the harvest of the lilies - and the secret, dark production of the jorogumo Akari - to survive. Following the violent confrontration where Shinzatsu slew Akari, the magical lilies instantly crumbled into ash, stripping the village of its only livelihood. Branded as monsters by the heartbroken locals and left in total poverty, Kumohana's population rapidly dispersed, leaving the valley a quiet, ghost-town remnant of its former prosperity.
