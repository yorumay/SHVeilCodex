A prosperous hot spring village nestled in a lush, highly fertile mountain valley high on the slopes of [[Daidake]]. Built around the sprawling complex of the [[Temple of the Red Fog]]. [[Akayu]] owes its vibrant agricultural yield and soothing thermal waters to the volcanic activity beneath the mountain. Though famous through out [[Tenmei]] as a sanctuary, the village serves as the operational heartland for the [[Temple of the Red Fog|Red Fog]], blending peaceful village life with fanatical devotion under the watchful eye of temple zealots.
