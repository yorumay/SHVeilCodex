A rich, strategically vital coastal province on the island of [[Shinkoku]], governed by the bamboo [[spiritfolk]] [[shugo-daimyo]] [[Katsuhara Omitsu]]. Dosaki is defined by the vast, ancient [[Waturi Forest]] and the bustling deep-water port city of [[Renkyu]], situated where the [[Tanokawa]] River meets the sea. Blessed with an endless supply of high-grade ship timber, Renkyu houses the renowned [[Imperial Shipyards]], constructing the largest naval war junks in the realm. In addition to its maritime dominance, [[Renkyu]] is home to [[Kozakura]]'s only official academy for [[wu jen]], making Dosaki a unique epicenter of naval might and arcane study.
