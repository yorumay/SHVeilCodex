A major port city strategically situated at the inner bend of the [[Inland Sea]]. Renkyu serves as the central naval hub and economic gateway connecting the eastern coastal provinces to the heartland of [[Kozakura]]. Fed by timber transported down the [[Tanokawa]], the city is renowned for its bustling shipyards, dry docks, and master shipwrights who construct both swift merchant junks and heavily armored war galleys for imperial and noble fleets. Its sheltered deep-water harbor makes it a crucial crossroads for sea trade, naval patrols, and foreign intrigue.
