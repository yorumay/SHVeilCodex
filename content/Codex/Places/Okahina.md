A coastal port city situated along the strategic strait separating the islands of Shinkoku and Tenmei. Following the catastrophe of the Bluefire, nearly half of Okahina subsided into the sea, leaving its lower districts five to ten feet underwater and wiping out both the local nobility and civic leadership. Vacuumed of authority, the city was seized by Kirishima Daito and his notorious Straw Hats pirate fleet. Declaring himself "Admiral," Daito instituted draconian taxes on all passing trade to fund massive engineering projects - rebuilding the seawall, repairing the harbor, and restoring the lighthouse to forge a self-sustaining, pirate-ruled haven.
