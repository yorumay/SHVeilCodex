A majestic, windswept sacred mountain overlooking the coastal city of Kaisei. Ancient legends declare that Fujin, the God of Wind, made his early home upon its craggy summits, making the peak a holy site where tengu warriors perform perilous climbing plgrimages to prove their devotion to the sky god. The mountain is also historically famous as the former lair of a venerable t'ien lung (celestial dragon), which was notoriously hunted down and slain by elite Shogunate forces forty years ago in a blood display of martial dominance.
