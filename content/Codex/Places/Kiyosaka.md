A coastal port city in [[Shizume]] Province, situated at the opposing shore of Hikariboshi Bay from [[Fukama]]. Though smaller than its regional neighbor, [[Kaisei]], Kiyosaka plays a vital role in provincial trade where the rugged [[Akagane-no-Oka]] sweep down into the ocean. The town is famous throughout the region for the wealth harvested from the mineral-rich highlands - notably heavy yields of copper, rare pigments, and mountain ores - which are loaded directly onto merchant junks from its busy harbor docks.
