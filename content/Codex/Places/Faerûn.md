A massive, highly diverse continent located in the western hemisphere of [[Toril]]. Home to famed metropolises such as Waterdeep and Baldur's Gate, Faerûn is a land of dense magic, ancient fallen empires, and endless planar portals. While distant from [[Kozakura]] by thousands of leagues across treacherous seas and vast deserts, bold traders, divine refugees and daring adventurers like [[Marabia Sorrow]] occasionally make the grueling multi-year trek between Faerûn and the Far East.
