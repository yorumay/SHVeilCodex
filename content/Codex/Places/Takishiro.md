## Key Facts
> **Location:** Northern Shizume, on the road from Horosawa
> **Garrison:** Permanent; standard complement
> **Purpose:** Northern approach defence; customs and road inspection
> **Status:** Active; garrison morale high but combat experience limited

Takishiro watches the northern approach from Horosawa, a province that is quiet on its own terms but serves as a corridor between Shizume and the provinces beyond. The fortress sits on a ridge above the road, built to command the downhill approaches rather than simply block the pass, giving its garrison early visibility of anyone moving south. The design reflects the province's priorities: Takishiro is meant to see trouble coming, not merely stop it after it arrives.

The posting is considered a respectable but unexciting assignment. Horosawa has no significant military force of its own, and the traffic through the northern pass is mostly merchants and seasonal travelers rather than soldiers. Takishiro's garrison spends more time managing customs disputes than conducting military exercises, a fact that its commanders alternately resent and quietly appreciate.
