The central world of the Material Plane, orbiting as the third planet from the sun within Realmspace. Toril is orbited by a single glowing moon, Selûne, which is trailed by a glittering cluster of pale asteroids known as the Tears of Selûne. The planet emcompasses vast, diverse landmasses - including [[Faerûn]], [[Kara-Tur]], and Zakhara - and serves as the primary mortal anchor point for countless divine pantheons, cosmic alignments, and planar connections across the Great Wheel.
