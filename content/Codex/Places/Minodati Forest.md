A dense, sprawling forest dominated by massive groves of towering bamboo, interspersed with thick stands of cypress and ancient cedar trees. Situated rirectly south of the coastal town of [[Sudaiko]], the forest stretches along the treacherous foothills of the centrla [[Dragon's Spine]] mountains. Due to its labyrinthine terrain and thick cover, the Minodati serves as a primary haven for outlaw bands, rogue yakuza syndicates, and masterless ronin evading provincial law enforcement.

Most notoriously, the forest is used as a seasonal stronghold by the infamous bandit chief Higemoto. Each winter, Higemoto's forces would cross the mountain passes to vanish deep into the dense cover of the Minodati; come spring, they would emerge from the treeline to ambush wealthy travelers and merchant caravans moving along the southern [[Imperial Post Road]].
