A prospresous, mid-sized coastal town situated within [[Iwari]] province on the main island of [[Shinkoku]]. Tucked into rolling coastal hills overlooking the ocean, Sudaiko owes its booming economy to the rich veins of precious gemstones extracted from the surrounding mountains. The town serves as both a busy trading post for lapidaries and jewel merchants, as well as a key coastal port. Situated directly north of the dense [[Minodati Forest]], Sudaiko's wealth makes its outbound roads and merchant caravans a frequent target for the outlaws and bandits who take refuge beneath the forest's bamboo canopy.
