One of the three major neighboring islands situated northwest of [[Tenmei]] that lie outside direct [[Kozakura]]n control while remaining firmly within its sphere of influence. Free of steep volcanic peaks, Kanshu is defined by rolling forested hills, cleft valleys, and a central spine of rugged, timbered ridges. Nearly the entire landmass is shrouded by the dense, untamed Chijoshi Forest, leaving little land for farming. Over the centuries, invaders from foreign shores have repeatedly tried to establish footholds here, only to be bloodily repelled by an alliance of human woodsmen, native bamboo spiritfolk, and elushive [[shan sao]]. Kanshu remains a fiercely independent wilderness, famously serving as the staging ground where the exiled warlord [[Hojo Todahiro]] gathered hundreds of retainers before launching his ill-fated final assault on [[Gizan]], Dozaki, and Ike.
