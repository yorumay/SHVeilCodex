A wealthy yet treacherous province whose landscape is defined by the [[Shibinuma]] - a vast, fog-shrouded wetland created centuries ago when a massive earthquake violently rerouted the Jito River. A trackless expanse of lethal quicksand, giant leeches, and monstrous swamp fauna, the [[Shibinuma]] is shunned by standard armies, though hermits seek isolation in its depths and local fishermen navigate its murky edges in flat-bottomed skiffs to harvest freshwater eels.

Directly across the Jito river from the wetland lies [[Jitogawa]], the thriving economic heart of Naredo. The city is dominated by the [[Silk Za]], a powerful merchant guild holding an absolute monopoly over [[Kozakura]]'s lucrative silk trade. Previously a [[Takenaka]] stronghold, [[Jitogawa]] is now firmly under [[bakufu|Shogunate]] control. However, official enforcement in the city is intentionally lax; the local [[daimyo]] [[Hojo Tsuneyo]], turns a blind eye to guild corruption in exchange for massive financial kickbacks, using the silk fortunes to quietly fund her own elite private garrison.
