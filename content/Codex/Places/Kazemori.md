*The Rusting Shield / Castle of the Dying Breeze*

A famous samurai garrison town that was once celebrated across the realm for training fierce, honorable swordsmen. Over the past decade, Kazemori has bitterly fallen from grace into isolation and decay. The town is ruled by Kuroda Matatsune, an aging, inward-looking minor daimyo who has allowed the garrison's traditions to rust while shielding his domain from outside conflicts. Following the death of his long-time wife last winter, the elderly Matatsune scandalized the local nobility by taking an alluring new wife - a charismatic former oiran named Koyuri, whose true ambitions and background remain a subject of quiet, suspicious whispers throughout the court.
