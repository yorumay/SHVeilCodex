A once-thriving trading hub nestles in the northern mountains along the upper stretches of teh Sakuragawa. Renowned across the province for its sprawling, open-air mineral bazaar - where prospectors, miners, and gem-cutters peddled raw ore and precious stones like fishmongers at a coastal port. Tragically, Hoshizuki was completely obliterated when the Bluefire tore through the mountain valley; today, nothing remains of the bustling town.
