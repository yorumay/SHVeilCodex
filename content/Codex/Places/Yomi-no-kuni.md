The dark, shadowy underworld where souls travel upon death, ruled by ancient chthonic deities and rotting spirits. A cold, cavernous realm of perpetual gloom and decay, Yomi is connected to the mortal plane by heavy stone seals and deep mountain caverns. Entering Yomi while still alive - or eating food cooked over its fires - pollutes a mortal with irreversible spiritual impurity (kegare) and binds their soul to the hall of shadows forever.
