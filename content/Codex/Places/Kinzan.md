A secretive, heavily guarded settlement located deep in the northern reaches of the Sakuragawa valley, inhabited by the korobokuru. Long ago, the winderness dwarves lived here in hidden isolation due to historical laws banning non-human residents from holding land. That changed when the forward-thinking Honda clan granted the korobokuru full citizenship and territorial rights. In exchange, Kinzan became the province's exclusive supplier of precious tamahagane - the legendary, high-purity folded steel required to forge elite samurai blades (katana) and heavy armor.
