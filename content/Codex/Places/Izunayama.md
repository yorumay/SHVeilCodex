A sacred, towering mountain in Shizume province named after the kami Izuna, who originally blessed its heights with celestial prophecies. Beneath its snow-capped peak lies a hollowed volcanic core - the ancestral home wher eIzuna and his consort, Fuyukimi, once dwelt. Two centuries ago, the mountain erupted in apocalyptic fire when the elemental Akujin tore through its roots, triggering a conflict that left the god dead, the yuki-onna imprisoned in ice, and the mountain's geothermal heart corrupted. It recently served as the epicenter of a province-wide blizzard until the divine rebirth of its patron spirits restored balance to the peak.
