The formidable western gate of [[Dojyu]], constructed from dark ironwood and heavy stone ramparts. In [[Kozakura]]n tradition, the west is associated with the setting sun, funeral rites, and the passage into the spirit realm. Consequently, the Sunset Gate serves as the functional exit for outgoing military garrisons, penal processions, and funeral processions traveling toward the western mountain cemetaries. While less ornate than its eastern counterpart, its shadow hosts a bustling, seedier district where travelers, mercenaries, and underworld fixers gather away from the watchful eyes of the high court officials.
