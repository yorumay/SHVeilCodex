The bustling capital of Tenmei Province, dramatically carved into a sheer volcanic fjord and governed by Shugo-Daimyo Suzuki Motosune. Twenty-two years ago, Motosune officially declared the Temple of the Red Fog - an esoteric, volcanic cult centered around the nearby active volcano Daidake - as the state religion of Yoshida. The town's architecture reflects this dark spiritual shift, with obsidian tiled roofs, sulfurous bathhouses, and crimson banners billowing through the sea mists.
