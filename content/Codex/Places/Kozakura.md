A vast, island nation defined by towering volcanic mountain ranges, mist-shrouded valleys, ancient cedar forests, and a rich cultural tradition of martial honor and spiritual reverence. Nominally united under the divine lineage of the Akimatsu Dynasty and its celestial capital in Dojyu, the realm is practically governed by regional military lords (shugo-daimyo) and their loyal samurai clans. It is a land where ancient kami dwell in sacred mountain groves, yokai lurk in forgotten ruins, and delicate arts like kabuki flourish alongside black-powder technology and lethal swordsmanship. Recently wracked by the catastrophic Bluefire, Kozakura stands at a perilous turning point - where ancient gods fall and mortal champions rise to shape its destiny.
