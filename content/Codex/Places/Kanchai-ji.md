The imposing esoteric temple of the Kanchai (Shingon/Tendai) school of the [[Way of Enlightenment]], located within [[Dojyu]]. Governed by the stern High Priest Gamihara, Kanchai-ji is famous for its rigorous ascetic rituals, fire ceremonies, complex mandala meditations, and deep study of secret doctrines. Gamihara maintains a vast network of scholar-monks and [[shukenja]] across [[Shinkoku]], positioning Kanchai-ji as a primary spiritial and political advisory council to high-ranking court aristocrats and traditional noble families.
