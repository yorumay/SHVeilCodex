The largest town and principal shipping hub on the island of [[Hinomoto]], located along the strategic [[Straits of Ama]]. Built around the naturally sheltered waters of Omitsu Bay - whose shallow coves host fleets of small fishing craft - Itzuhachi serves as a vital stopover for merchant junks, naval patrols, and pilgrims traveling across the archipelago. As the provincial administrative central and seat of the [[shugodai]], the town's harbor guards enforce strict customs checks and maintain the imperial weapon ban within municipal borders.
