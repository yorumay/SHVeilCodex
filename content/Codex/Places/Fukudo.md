A rugged, strategically vital province dominated by volcanic geography, dense woodland, and mountain passes. Fukudo is argually the most economically critical territory for the Shogunate's war effort: its expansive sulfur mines supply the essential ingredient required to produce gunpowder for the realm's growing arsenal of [[tanegashima]] matchlock rifles. The province's dark recent history includes the Shadow War, a bloody conflict where the cunning young Hojo Kawabuko utilized ninja clans to ruthlessly eliminate the ruling [[Takenaka]] clan and secure the territory for the [[bakufu|Shogunate]].
