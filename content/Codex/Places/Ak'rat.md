A mythical, cloud-shrouded sanctuary drifting silently in the high winds above the Akagane-no-Oka mountain range. Virtually unknown to the lowland mortals of Kozakura, Ak'rat serves as the secretive stronghold of the avian tengu, who resettled its magnificent stone towers and gravity-defying plazas centuries ago.
