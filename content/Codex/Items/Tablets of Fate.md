Two massive, inscribed stone slabs created by [[Ao]] at the dawn of Realmspace to record the exact names, boundaries, and responsibilities of every deity in the pantheon. Far more than mere historical record, the Tablets served as the metaphysical foundation anchoring divine portfolios to the gods. When [[Bane]] and [[Myrkul]] stole them in 1358 DR - believing the stones themselves granted omnipotence - they severed the divine order, forcing [[Ao]] to banish the gods to [[Toril]] until the shattered remnants were recovered and destroyed.
