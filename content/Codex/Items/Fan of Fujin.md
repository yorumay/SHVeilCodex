Once the possession of [[Fujin]], [[kami|god]] of wind, this fan was taken by the [[tengu]], who held it for centuries as the symbol of their divine right to rule the skies. When the tengu moved to ally with the mortals of [[Fukama]] against the [[bakufu|shogunate]], the ninja of [[Kazehaya]] stole it. They broke its wards in doing so, and [[Fujin]] recalled the fan in a tornado that tore across the countryside. When the [[Shinzatsu]] later stood before the [[kami|god]] himself, he offered the fan to them a as a sign of trust between the [[amatsukami]] and [[nakatsukuni|mortals]].
