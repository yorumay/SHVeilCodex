## Background
The Hisakawa did not begin as the Hisakawa. Their true lineage is Kurogawa - a ninja clan that served as the Honda regents faithfully until the Hojo coup of 1045 DR. When the Hojo moved against Honda, Kurogawa were branded traitors. A unified force of samurai and ninja came for them in their village, and the clan was destroyed.

Some survived. Tehy scattered, changed their name, and spent two centuries becoming something that didn't look like a ninja clan anymore. By the time they re-emerged as the Hisakawa, they had traded their forest village for a provincial seat, their black gear for the robes of minor nobility, and their open allegiance for a network of influence that runs through Kozakura's courts like water through stone.

Chibana province is nothing much on its own - rocky terrain that produces little of value. But it sits at the junction of roads connecting the southern Kozakura to Dojyu and the Chimotoge pass. Every caravan, every courier, every whispered message traveling the empire's most important trade routes pass through Chibana, and the clan has made good use of that fact.
