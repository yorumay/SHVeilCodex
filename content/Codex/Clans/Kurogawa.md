A legendary former [[ninja]] clan whose fate serves as a grim historical warning across [[Kozakura]]. Following the end of the [[Tennu War]] in 1045 DR - when the captured [[tenno|Emperor]] [[Showaji]] was forced to declare [[Hojo Tademashi]] as [[Shogun]] - the new shogunate officially outlawed all [[ninja]] clans and launched a ruthless scorched-earth campaign against their strongholds. The Kurogawa's ancestral hidden village in the [[Minodati Forest]] of [[Sogai]] Province was burned to ash and the house was officially recorded as eradicated.

In complete secrecy, however, the bloodline survived. Reduced to a single surviving family, the clan adopted the name [[Hisakawa]] to conceal their identity from shogunal inquisitors while quietly maintaining their sacred oath of service to the [[Honda]] Clan.
