A once-venerated line of itinerant physicians and herbalists who spent generations wandering the roads of Kozakura to treat commoners, peasants, and the destitute without care for coin or status. The clan came to a catastrophic end nearly three decades ago (in 1356 DR) in Fukudo Province, where they were discovered providing medical aid, food, and shelter to escaped orcish slaves fleeing the sulfur mines. Accused of high treason, the Shogunate imprisoned the family, stripped their clan name, and systematically expunged their house from official historical rolls. To the military government, the Saejima remain branded as "Traitors to the State who aided non-human insurgents" - though among the low-born folk of the countryside, quiet whispers still honor their sacrifice.
