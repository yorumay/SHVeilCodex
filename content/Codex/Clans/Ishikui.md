

A tight-knit, proud family of korobokuru residing in the high alpine village of Toturi. Rooted deep in the roots of the peaks, the clan is led by the venerable patriarch Ishikui Iwao and his matriarch wive Ishikui Ishino. Taking inspiration from the colossal, enduring mountain towering above their home, the couple famously named their children in simple numerical order using the suffix -dai ("generation" / "big"): Ichidai (first), Nidai (second), Sandai (third), Yondai (fourth), Godai (fifth), and Rokudai (sixth). Beyond their quarrying and metallurgy, the clan carries the sacred obligation of guarding the subterranean depths beneath their mountain.
