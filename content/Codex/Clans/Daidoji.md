A minor, short-lived samurai line elevated solely through the distinguished service of its founder, Daidoji Razan. Granted the family name and the stewardship of Takeo as a lifelong reward, the title was never intended to be hereditary. By decree, the estate and administrative duties were meant to revert to the provincial authority upon Razan's death, with his family relocated to new lands. However, due to administrative oversights and shifting regional conflicts, the decree was never executed - leaving the Daidoji name unexpectedly tethered to Takeo for a second generation.
