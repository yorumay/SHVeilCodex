A terrifyingly effective [[ninja]] clan that secretly survived the widespread purges following [[Tennu War]] in 1045 DR. While rival shadow houses were burned to ash and erased from history, the Uji adapted, offering their unquestioning, lethal service to the rising Hojo clan. Over the centuries, they have grown into the [[bakufu|Shogunate]]'s most heavily guarded secret - an indespensable instrument of state terror whose ancestral strongholds, techniques, and training methods remain completely unknown to outsiders.

Operating primarily out of the shogunal capital of [[Gifu]], the Uji are infamous throughout the realm as silent, inescapable executioners. It was Uji who formed the vanguard of [[Hojo Kawabuko]]'s night of slaughter during [[Highharvestide]], striking fifteen [[Takenaka]] fortresses simultaneously to inaugurate the [[Shadow War]]. Today, the mere whisper of the Uji name is enough to silence dissent among court nobles and regional [[daimyo]] alike.
