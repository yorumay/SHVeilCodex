An ancient, highly respected noble lineage originally hailing from the imperial capital of Dojyu. Possessing a prestigious lineage that traces its ancestral roots directly back to the 17th Emperor, the Matsudaira have been closely bound to the Honda clan through marriage and political alliance for two generations. Their ancient imperial blood grants them immense diplomatic respect among both the old aristocracy and the rising Imperial Restoration.
