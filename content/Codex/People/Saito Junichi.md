A deadly mortal assassin who once served as the Imperial Court's primary hitokiri (manslayer). Captured by the Shogunate three years ago and condemned to execution, Junichi mysteriously vanished from his high-security cell - rescued by the dark, chaotic influence of a foreign deity Cyric. He has since reemerged at bizarre, grisly murder scenes across the realm. The penal tattoos inked into his flesh by his Shogunate captors now writhe with unnatural life, while the terrifying mark of Cyric completely covers his back. Twisted by divine madness, Junichi operates as an unpredictable, violent wild card whose survivors describe him as utterly unhinged.
