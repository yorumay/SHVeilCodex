The practical, sharp-witted matriarch of the Ishikui family whose steady hand keeps both the household and village affairs running smoothly while her husband watches the depths. Her name - meaning "of the stone" - reflects her foundational role in the clan. Ishino is the primary force behind the family's unique naming tradition, having firmly insisted on numbering their six children (Ichidai, Nidai, Sandai, Yondai, Godai and Rokudai) in honor of the great mountain's shadow under which they were raised.
