The grim, calculating god of death, decay, old age, and the fear of dying. Alongside [[Bane]], Myrkul conspired to steal the [[Tablets of Fate]], inadvertently triggering the Avatar Crisis. Cold and sadistic, Myrkul ruled the City of the Dead with iron cruelty - tormenting the souls of the Faithless in the Wall of the Faithless - until his mortal avatar was destroyed by the wizard [[Midnight]] during the climactic battles of 1358 DR. Even in death, remnants of his necromantic will linger inside cursed artifacts like the Crown of Horns.
