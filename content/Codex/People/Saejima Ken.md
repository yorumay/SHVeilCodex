The last known heir of the disgraced [[Saejima]] Clan. Following his family's fal nearly three decades ago for treating escaped orc slaves, Ken was dragged in chains to the grueling sulfur mines of [[Fukudo]] Province. He later vanished under bizarre, unexplained circumstances - leaving behind only his severed arm in the dark tunnels.

Surviving his escape, Ken was taken in by the legendary druid master Akiyama, spending decades in seclusion to master the primal traditions of nature. Together, master and pupil traversed the highways and wild frontiers of Kozakura, quietly aiding the destitute and sealing corrupted fissures spilling forth horrors from Yomi-no-Kuni. Now affectionately known to his allies as "Ken-ha," he has joined forces with the Shinzatsu adventuring party, lending his primal magic, ancient medical knowledge, and unyielding spirit to cleanse Kozakura of the darkness threatening to consume it.
