A pious young woman who passed away shortly after her twentieth year following an agonizing, year-long illness. Despite her frail physical body, Yomogi became the spiritual voice and central prophetess of the [[Temple of the Red Fog]]. During her prolonged affliction, she began exhibiting uncanny, unexplainable insight into [[hellenism|foreign]] [[yomi-no-kuni|underworld]] lore - preaching the rites of the [[Hellenism|Hellenistic]] deities with innate authority and demonstrating what her followers believed to be direct, unfettered commune with the underworld powers.
