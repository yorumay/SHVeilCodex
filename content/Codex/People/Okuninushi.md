A major, revered kami of the Kozakuran pantheon associated with the earth, medicine, nation-building, and unseen magic. Worshiped extensively by farmers, healers, and earth-mages alike, Okuninushi is regarded as the ancient divine ruler who cultivated the physical land before yielding political rule to hte heavenly descendants of the Sun. His sacred sites often overlap with ancient earth-nodes and powerful subterranean ley lines.
