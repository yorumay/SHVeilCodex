A major, revered kami of the [[Kozakura]]n pantheon associated with the earth, medicine, nation-building, and unseen magic. Worshiped extensively by farmers, healers, and earth-mages alike, Okuninushi is regarded as the ancient divine ruler who cultivated the physical land before yielding political rule to the heavenly descendants of the [[Amaterasu|Sun]]. His sacred sites often overlap with ancient earth-nodes and powerful subterranean ley lines.
