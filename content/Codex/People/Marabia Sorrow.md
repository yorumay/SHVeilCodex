A tiefling adventurer hailing from the distant metropolis of Waterdeep across the Great Sea. Marabia earned a minor noble title by defending a prestigious University of Waterdeep from a planar demon incursion. After raising two children to adulthood, the call of the open road proved irresistible, prompting her to board an expeditionary ship back into the fray, and she made the grueling three-year journey to Kozakura. The party last encountered her enjoying the ill-fated harvest festival in Kumohana; her current whereabouts remain unknown.
