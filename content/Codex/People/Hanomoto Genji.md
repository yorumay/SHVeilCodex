The revered and charismatic high leader of the [[Temple of the Red Fog]], who transformed the mountain sanctuary into a formidable political and religious movement across [[Tenmei]]. Highly respected by his follwoers for his formidable spiritual influence, Genji orchestrated the subversion of local rule in [[Tenmei]] and dictated cult doctrine from his own seat at [[Akayu]]. His reign came to a sudden, violent end within his own temple, where he was slain [[Saito Junichi]] during a fateful confrontation with the [[Shinzatsu]].
