The former high abbot of the Hoshikage Monastery who led his brotherhood into dark heresy. Obsessed with heat and survival as a supernatural winter tightened its grip over the province, Gen'un embraced the fiery whispers of Akujin's relic. He reinterpretated the monastery's doctrine, falsely preaching that the Cinder Lord was simply a new, fiery incarnation of their lost god Izuna - a deity of "fire and love." Gen'un's fanaticism culminated in a desperate attempt to unleash the elemental into the world, only for his hubris to backfire when the Obsidian Heart devoured him to fuel Akujin's brief, fatal return.
