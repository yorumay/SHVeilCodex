The aging vilalge headman (nanushi) of Kumohana, burdened by a crushing secret. Having uncovered Akari's true identity as a shapeshifting jorobumo, MAtsuo chose quiet complicity over destruction, rationalizing that her monstrous appetite was a small price to pay for security, wealth, and health she brought to his isolated people. His moral compromise cost him everything when Akari seized his daughter, Natsuko. Broken by the loss of his child and the sudden collapse of the village's prosperity, a grief-stricken Matsuo remained loyal to the memory of their spider patron - leading the enraged villagers to brand the party as heartless monsters and banish them from Kumohana.
