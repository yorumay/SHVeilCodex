An ancient, cosmic nightmare birthed by The Void to swallow all light, thought, and hope. Dendar manifests as an colossal, night-black serpent with sloughing scales and a maw capable of swallowing mountains. She slumbers beneath the roots of the world, growing ever larger by feeding on unmade nightmares and fears of all living mortals. Legend warns that if she eats enough nightmares, she will awaken to swallow the sun itself.
