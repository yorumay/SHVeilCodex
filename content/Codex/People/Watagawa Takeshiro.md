The ruthless, highly intelligence crime lord who commands the [[Dark Cloud]] gang - the undisputed syndicate dominating the shadowy alleys and underworld of [[Gifu]]. Operating right beneath the nose of the [[Shogun]]'s garrison, Takeshiro controls extortion, illegal gambling, smuggling, and black-market trade across the shogunate capital. Cunning and deeply connected, he maintains his bloody empire through a mix of lethal enforcement and well-placed bribes among court guards and local officials.
