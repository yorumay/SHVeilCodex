A legendary figure of dwarf-kind ([[korobokuru]]) whose exploits are woven into alpine folklore. During ancient times of upheaval, Maasate led his clan on a perilous trek across the sea to settle the rugged wilderness of [[Tenmei]]. Among the [[korobokuru]], he is remembered as master survivalist, stonemason, and fearless trailblazer who established the ancestral footholds his people inhabit to this day.
