The dark, primordial deity of night, secrets, loss, and the [[kage-no-sekai|Shadowfell]], standing as the eternal cosmic rival and twin sister to [[Selûne (Deity)|Selûne]]. Shar thrives in total darkness, bitter grief, and forgotten truths, seeking to unravel creation and draw all existence into cold, silent oblivion. While her open worship is outlawed across most civilized lands, her shadowy presence is felt in deep subterranean chasms, hidden cult sanctuaries, and the dark hearts of those consumed by despair or vengeful grudges. In eastern lands like [[Kozakura]], scholars often associate her malevolent domain with the dark aspects of the [[yomi-no-kuni|Underworld]] and corrupted [[yokai]] spirits of the night.
