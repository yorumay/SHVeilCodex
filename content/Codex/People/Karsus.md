A strange, foreign name spoken in an ancient tongue, revealed to Shinzatsu during a chilling vision deep within the crushed ruins beneath Akagane-no-Oka mountains. To scholars and gods of Kozakura, the name carries no meaning; but to scholars from Faerun tell the tale of ultimate hubris - the archmage whose single moment of unimaginable foolishness tore down an empire of floating cities over two millennia ago.
