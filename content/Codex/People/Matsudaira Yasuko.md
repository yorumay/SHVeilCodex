The formidable matriarch of the Matsudaira lineage who currently serves as the official Lady Regent for the province of Shizume. Yasuko's life was shattered by the tragic events of the Bluefire, which claimed her son (Shugo-Daimyo Honda Katsuhiro), her second son, Katsuhiro's wife, adn two of her grandsons in a single sweeping tragedy. Rising above her grief to protect her family's legacy, she now firmly guides the province and protects her nine-year-old granddaughter, Aoi, from political vultures and Shogunate plotters.
