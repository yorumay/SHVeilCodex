The formidable matriarch of the [[Matsudaira]] lineage who currently serves as the official Lady Regent for the province of [[Shizume]]. Yasuko's life was shattered by the tragic events of the [[Bluefire]], which claimed her son ([[Shugo-Daimyo]] [[Honda Katsuhiro]]), her second son, Katsuhiro's wife, and two of her grandsons in a single sweeping tragedy. Rising above her grief to protect her family's legacy, she now firmly guides the province and protects her nine-year-old granddaughter, [[Honda Aoi|Aoi]], from political vultures and [[bakufu|Shogunate]] plotters.
