![[Gond-1786607218097.webp|256]]The patron deity of invention, crafting, smithing, and engineering. Gond inspires artisans, blacksmiths, clockmakers, and siege engineers to push the boundaries of technology and physical creation. In Kozakura, master swordsmiths, armorers, and castle builders who revere Gond often blend his divine spark of innovation with local rites dedicated to Kagutsuchi, producing legendary weapons and complex mechanical traps.
