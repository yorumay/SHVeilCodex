The legendary divine domain and workshop of [[Gond|Gond the Wonderbringer]], revered across the planes as the ultimate mecca of invention, engineering, and craftsmanhip. Master crafters from every corner of the realms journey in desparate hope of gaining admission to its sacred halls to study under divine tutelage. The vast majority of applicants are turned away at the gates; only a select few are accepted by [[Gond]]'s mortal apprentices and his ancient guardian. While [[Gond]] himself is usually too consumed by cosmic creation to instruct newcomers directly, the daily operations and mentorship are overseen by his trusted apprentices and his long-time companion, the ancient copper dragon [[Ordalane]].
