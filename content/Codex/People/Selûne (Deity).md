The benevolent deity of the [[Selûne (Celestial Object)|moon]], stars, navigation, and good-aligned lycanthropes, widely revered by sailors, travelers, and nighttime wanderers across the western realms. Selûne embodies the gentle, guiding light that pierces the dark, waging a perpetual cosmic battle against her twin sister, [[Shar]], the goddess of darkness, secret loss, and forgetfulness. In foreign lands like [[Kozakura]], her domain mirrors that of local nocturnal deities such as [[Tsukuyomi]], with scholars and mystics often drawing parallels between her silver radiance and eastern teachings regarding the serene, watchful sky.
