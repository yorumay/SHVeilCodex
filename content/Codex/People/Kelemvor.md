A former mortal mercenary and companion to Midnight and Cyric who ascended to divinity in the aftermath of the Avatar Crisis. Claiming the portfolio of the dead from a defeated Cyric, Kelemvor overthrew the tyrannical legacy of Myrkul and transformed the Fugue Plane into a realm of solemn, impartial justice. Unlike his predecessors, Kelemvor teaches that death is a natural, peaceful end rather than something to be feared, judging departed souls fairly by their deeds rather than inflicing cruel torment.
