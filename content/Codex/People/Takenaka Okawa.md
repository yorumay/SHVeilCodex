The maternal grandfather of [[Hojo Kawabuko]] and the cunning politician who served as [[shikken]] (regent shogun) until his assassination in 1357 DR. A master of courtly administration, Okawa consolidated his grip over [[Kozakura]] through strategic land grants, tax dispensations, and noble patronage. However, his poltiical brilliance was matched by a severe lack of military acumen and fatal arrogance - leaving him entirely blind to the fact that his "puppet" grandson was secretly forging alliances with rival [[ninja]] clans right under his nose.
