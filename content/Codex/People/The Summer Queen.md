The supreme ruler of the Seelie Fey Court in the Feywild. A being of dazzling, blinding beauty and immense primordial magic, Titania embodies the vibrant warmth, growth, and sudden ruthlessness of midsummer. While distant from the daily political struggles of Kozakura, her fey emissaries and seasonal courts touch the hidden wild places, ancient groves, and bamboo forests of the realm.
