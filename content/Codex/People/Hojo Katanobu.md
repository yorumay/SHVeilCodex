A ruthless, veteran commander of the Hojo clan and cousin to Shogun Hojo Kawabuko. Appointed as the garrison lord of Chimotoge following the bloody "Shadow War," Katanobu governs the pass with cold, military efficiency. His sole instructions from Gifu are absolute: keep the Imperial Post Road open, crush any embers of the Imperial Restoration rebellion, and ensure the black powder sulfur caravans reach the Shogun's arsenals without delay.
