The ruthless, iron-fisted god of tyranny, hatred, and fear. Bane demands absolute obedience from his followers and seeks total domination over mortal realms through military might, systemic oppression, and cold calculation. Locked in a bitter, eternal divine rivalry with [[Cyric]] - who once usurped his portfolio during the [[Time of Troubles]] - Bane and his Black Network of priests, tyrants, and Zhentarim agents wage a relentless shadow war against Cyric's faithful across the planes.
