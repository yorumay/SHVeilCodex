The tragic sovereign whose reign marked the end of direct imperial rule in [[Kozakura]]. During his turbulent court, the ambitious [[Hojo]] clan executed a coup, and seized true administrative and military power. In 1045 DR, at the conclusion of the [[Tennu War]], a helpless Showaji was forced to issue an imperial decree conferring the title of Sei-i Taishogun upon [[Hojo Tademashi]]. This historic capitulation stripped the [[Chrysanthemum Throne]] of its ruling authority, reducing the [[tenno|Emperor]] and his descendants to symbolic and divine figure heads while [[shogun|military warlords]] assumed control of the realm.
