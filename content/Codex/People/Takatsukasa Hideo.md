The young heir to the immense wealth and lands of the prestigious [[Takatsukasa]] family, and a distant blood relative to [[Shogun]] [[Hojo Kawabuko]]. Nestled amidst the sprawling terraced fields of [[Majima]] in [[Tairayama]] Province, the [[Takatsukasa]] has served as an unwavering pillar of financial and political support for the [[bakufu|Shogunate]] for centuries. High-bred, immensely wealthy, and fiercely loyal to [[Gifu]], Hideo represents the next generation of shogunal aristocracy.
