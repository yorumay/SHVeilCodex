An ancient, revered crane-hengeyokai and one of the last living grandmasters of the esoteric divination (Onmyodo). Having lived for centuries, Lady Jiko-no-Tan is among the extremely rare few alive who were initiated into the art via the sacred Imperial Rites. Her mastery over elemental balancing, talismans (ofuda), stellar alignment, and spirit-binding makes her an invaluable repository of forgotten lore, serving as a quiet bridge between the mortal court and the ancient spiritual realm.
