The young daughter of Village Elder Matsuo, whose tragic fate sealed the ruin of Kumohana. When the party intervened during hte harvest festival and slew the bandits Akari had marked for her ritual consumption, the enraged jorogumo seized Natsuko as a replacement sacrifice to sustain her dark power. Though Shinzatsu pursued the monster into her lair and struck Akari down, they arrived too late to save Natsuko. Her death remains a grim reminder of the collateral damage left in the wake of heroics.
