![[Akari-1786608414509.webp|256]]A centuries-old jorogumo who abandoned indiscriminate hunting to construct a farm more grand, insidious domain. Masking her monstrous nature beneath the serene guise of a devoted shrine maiden, Akari spent generations protecting the isolated village of Kumohana. She preyed exclusively upon the bandits, vagabonds, and outlaws who threatened her flock, secretly using her dark magic to transmute their remains into fertile soil where precious white spider lilies bloomed. Her ultimate goal was divine ascension - cultivating genuine love and worship from the populace to transform herself into the true Kumo-no-Kami. Though she genuinely safeguarded the village, her monstrous nature reemerged when her grand harvest ritual was disrupted, leading her to kidnap young Natsuko and ultimately fall to the blades of Shinzatsu.
