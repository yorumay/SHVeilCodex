Originally a gifted, passionate mortal wizard from the city of Deepingdale who played a pivotal role in recovering the stolen Tablets of Fate during the Time of Troubles. When the previous incarnation of the goddess of magic was slain by Helm while trying to breach the celestial stairways, Ao offered her divine portfolio to Midnight. Ascending to godhood, she took on the name and mantle of Mystra, striving to maintain the balance and accessibility of the Weave.
