A senior [[roju|councilor]] of the [[Bakufu|Shogunate]] and the cold, calculating younger cousin of [[Shogun]] [[Hojo Kawabuko|Kawabuko]]. Infamous for his sadistic cruelty and tactical genius, Sadamune covertly invaded and occupied Honda clan's strategic city of [[Kaisei]]. His tyrannical rule ended abruptly when [[Shinzatsu]] infiltrated his stronghold and assassinated him in his private bathhouse.
