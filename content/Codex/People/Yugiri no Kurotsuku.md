One of the legendary thirteen nagpa - the ancient, vulture like sorcerers cursed by the Raven Queen for their unspeakable betrayal. Kurotsuku is particularly infamous among the Shadowfell's agents for his uncanny ability to evade capture across centuries. Whispers among planar arcanists suggest he has unlocked master-level secrets of chronomancy, allowing him to manipulate the flow of time, slip past temporal fissures, and manifest at critical historical turning points through out past and present.
