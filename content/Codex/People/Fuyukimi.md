An ancient yuki-onna whose immortal grief and vengeance nearly consumed the realm. Originally softened from a cold monster into a loving spouse by the god Izuna, Fuyukimi was tragically mistaken for a monster by the panicked monks of Hoshikage during Akujin's initial eruption and sealed inside a massive pillar of ice. Trapped for two centuries while the monks erased her from memory, her agonizing sorrow bled into the sky, building a province-spanning blizzard that summoned legions of yuki-no-ko. When Shinzatsu slew Akujin and guided the snow children to her prison, they freed her. Learning the truth of the monks' tragic mistake and reunited with Izuna's lingering spirit, Fuyukimi let go of her wrath - fusing with Izuna to ascend into the Takamagahara as a new Amatsukami of Snow and Stars, instantly clearing the catastrophic storm.
