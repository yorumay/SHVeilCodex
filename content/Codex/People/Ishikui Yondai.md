> "The breaker must bind what the darkness freed, lest the stone dissolve in the Hound's own greed."

The Ishikui have been stonewatchers since before most of Tenmei's written history - a family that understood, in a way most korobokuru prefer not to think about, that somethign went wrong in the deep mines generations ago. They had been managing the fact quietly ever since.

Yondai slipped into those mines as a child. What came out of the crack below touched him and the weave bent around him in ways it shouldn't - ways that took decades of disciplined study to achieve, and that he achieved at age of however-old-he-was without study at all.

His clan called it corruption. An embarrasment. A stain on the Ishikui legacy of careful, controlled stonewatching. They weren't wrong. He was changed. He left - or was made to leave, depending on who tells the tale - with the intention of becoming something worth returning as. The heroic legends of what the korobokuru used to be are the measuring stick he held himself against. Whether that measuring stick fits what he actually became is a question the scholars are still answering.
