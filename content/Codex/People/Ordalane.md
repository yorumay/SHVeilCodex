An ancient copper dragon over 6,000 years of age who has made the Wonderhome her sanctuary for the past five millenia. Having spent her tempestous youth in rebellion against cosmic expectations, Ordanale settled down following the death of her mother - who served as Gond's companion before perishing in an ancient war between the gods. Whimsical, sharpwitted, and deeply devoted to the craft, Ordalane assists in vetting and watching over Gond's mortal students. She maintains a wry family dynamic; her sister, Balarystul, serves directly under Bahamut, prompting endless jests from Gond about Balarystul moonlighting as a barber in some fiery infernal plane.
