![[Shi'rokha-1786608002936.webp|256]]The venerable leader of the tengu delegation from the floating city of Ak'rat, and a lifelong personal friend of the late Shugo-Daimyo Honda Katsuhiro. During the Festival of Divine Descent in Fukama, Shi'rokha took a momentous, dangerous stand: before a grand assembly of samurai, Shinzatsu, and Tengu warriors, he and Katsuhiro publicly pledged their armies to the Emperor. This historic alliance formally launched the Imperial Restoration, firing the opening salvo to strip power from the Shogun.
