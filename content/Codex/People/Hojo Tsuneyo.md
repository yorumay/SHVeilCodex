A sharp, calculating noblewoman and distant kinswoman to [[Shogun]] [[Hojo Kawabuko]]. Tsuneyo is one of the rare female daimyo in [[Kozakura]] to hold direct provincial rule under the [[Hojo]] crest. Appointed to secure [[Naredo]] following the downfall of the [[Takenaka]], she eschews rigid moral posturing in favor of pragmatic, high-stakes economics. By granting the powerful [[Silk Za]] complete commercial freedom in [[Jitogawa]], Tsuneyo secures immense wealth through secret kickbacks, using the funds to equip and impeccably trained personal army loyal first and foremost to her house.
