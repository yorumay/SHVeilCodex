The 68-year-old [[daimyo]] governing the province of [[Kazemori]]. Matatsune is a mild-mannered, steady, and largely unremarkable ruler who has maintained a quiet, stable rule over his domain for more than four decades. Though long considered a predictable fixture of regional politics, he recently scandalized local noble courts by taking a captivating traveling [[oiran]] named [[Koyuri (Oiran)|Koyuri]] as his new wife - a sudden late-life marriage that has raised curious whispers among rival houses and watchful advisers alike.
