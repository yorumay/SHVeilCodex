The current patriarch of the Ishikui family and the reigning Stonewatcher of Toturi. Stubborn as the mountain granite and fiercely protective of his clan, Iwao is a veteran quarrymaster who has spent his entire life studying the shifts, echoes, and strange tremors of the deep mines. His name - meaning "rock/stone man" - fits his unyielding demeanor. Though gruff and slow to trust outsiders, his authority in Toturi is undisputed, and he carries the burden of keeping the dark entitites of the underworld sealed away with solemn pride.
