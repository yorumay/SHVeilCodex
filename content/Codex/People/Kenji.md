An enigmatic elemental sorcerer ([[wu jen]]) of unknown clan origins who served as the chief arcane advisor to the sadistic councilor [[Hojo Sadamune]]. A master of subtle magic and dark wards, Kenji maintained [[Hojo Sadamune|Sadamune]]'s hold over the occupied city of [[Kaisei]]. Following the assassination of [[Hojo Sadamune|Sadamune]] in his private bathhouse by [[Shinzatsu]], Kenji vanished without a trace, leaving his current whereabouts, loyalties, and master plans entirely unknown.
