An enigmatic elemental sorcerer (wu jen) of unknown clan origins who served as the chief arcane advisor to the sadistic councilor Hojo Sadamune. A master of subtle magic and dark wards, Kenji maintained Sadamune's hold over the occupied city of Kaisei. Following the cover assassination of Sadamune in his private bathhouse by Shinzatsu, Kenji vanished without a trace, leaving his current whereabouts, loyalties, and master plans entirely unknown.
