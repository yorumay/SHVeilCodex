The 9th ruler of [[Kozakura]] and the first Empress to ascend the [[Chrysanthemum Throne]] in her own right. Beginning her historic sixty year reign in 430 DR, Empress Jumei commissioned the construction of the imperial grand capital of [[Dojyu]] in 431 DR. Remembered as an era of cultural transformation, unprecedented architectural ambition, and administrative reform, her long reign laid the foundational traditions of the [[Imperial Court]] that endure to this day.
