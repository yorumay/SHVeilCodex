A synthetic religious movement engineered by the jorogumo Akari to exploit the spiritual mechanics of the realm. Operating on the principle that collective mortal belief can elevate a powerful yokai into a genuine kami, the faith was built upon tangible divine "miracles" - most notably the miraculous blooming of white spider lilies fed by the transmuted bodies of slain bandits. To the villagers of Kumohana, Kumo-no-Kami was a benevolent, protective spirit of prosperity and health. The faith met a sudden, catastrophic end when Akari's death caused every white spider lily in the valley to instantly crumble into black ash.
