The former emperor of [[Kozakura]] who retired from the throne in 1352 DR, passing the imperial title to his fifteen-year-old son, [[Gonijo]]. Rather than fading into quiet monastic life, Gokammu has used his unconstrained status as Dajo Tenno (Retired Emperor) to serve as his son's primary advisor and co-conspirator in the shadows. Operating out of the First Ward in [[Dojyu]], Gokammu is a master strategist known for executing bold political appointments - such as placing his fierce loyalist, [[Ashigi Desugaki]], as governor of [[Iwari]] Province to establish a strategic imperial bastion in [[Augiyama Castle]].
