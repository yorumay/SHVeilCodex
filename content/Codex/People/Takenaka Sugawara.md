A legendary military genius and half-brother to Takenaka Okawa. Sugawara was the beloved hero of the Hojo War, leading pro-Kawabuko forces to victory and sealing the conflict in 1347 DR witha brilliant naval triumph over Hojo Todahiro off the island of Norinishima. Despite his immense popularity and military brilliance, Sugawara lacked the bloodline claim required for the regency, which fell to his half-brother Okawa. When the internal cold war fractured the clan, Sugawara led the Northwestern Takenaka in opposition. He met his tragic end on the fateful night of Highharvestide in 1357 DR, cut down in the opening strikes of the Shadow War.
