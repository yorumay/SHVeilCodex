A stauch partisan of the Imperial Court personally appointed by Retired Emperor Gokammu to govern Iwari province. Operating from the heavily fortified Augiyama Castle, Desugaki serves as a vital political anchor and imperial bastion designed to disrupt the Shogunate influecne on the region. While widely praised as a brilliant administrator and economic manager, he is notoriously unsuited for battlefield command - a weakness his enemies in the military factions actively seek to exploit.
