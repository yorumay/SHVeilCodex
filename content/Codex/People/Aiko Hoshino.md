A former maiko whose early life was defined by hardship after beign sold to an okiya at age five and cast onto the streets ten years alter. Refusing to succumb to poverty, Aiko answered the regional call for brave souls and joined Shinzatsu on their fateful first oni hunt against Bokyo. During her time with the guild, she formed a deep bond with Ryuji Saito, eventually retiring from field adventuring when he re-entered the Daimyo's service. Today, Aiko serves as the respected Matron of Gintaka-no-ie, skillfully managing the guild's staff, finances, and day-to-day operations.
