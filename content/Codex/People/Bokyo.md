Originally a merciful kami of forgetfulness, Bokyo was once revered by mortals who traveled to his mountain shrine to lay down unfixable tools and shed the crushing grief of lost loved ones. Over centuries, his remote sanctuary fell into a mountain ravine and was buried in stone, causing his worship to vanish. Soured and corrupted by mortal abandonment, Bokyo warped into a bitter shiro-oni - guiding local goblin tribes to raid nearby settlements. His fallen reign came to a decisive end when the newly formed Shinzatsu struck him down in his ruined shrine, marking the guild's very first act as adventurers.
