*The Blood-Fox*

A malevolent, vampiric variant of a kitsune known as a hu hsien, who feeds on the vital soul-energy (ki) of mortals. Years ago, after a deadly confrontation with the former samurai Okamura Renjishi, the two forged a dark, binding pact: He would deliver the souls of slain yokai to feed her insatiable hunger, whole Koyuri swore to grant him dark material strength and leave the town of Kazemori and his family untouched.

In recent times, however, the cosmic terms of their pact have inexplicably inverted. Rather than flowing to Koyuri, the harvested soul-stuff now feeds and empowers Guroutsu directly - leaving the ancient fox-spirit weakened, starving, and consumed by a terrifying, vengeful fury.
