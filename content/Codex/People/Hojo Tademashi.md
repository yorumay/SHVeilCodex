The historical architect of military rule in [[Kozakura]]. Following his victory in the [[Tennu War]] in 1045 DR, Tademashi compelled the captured [[tenno|Emperor]] [[Showaji]] to grant him the supreme military title of *Sei-i Taishogun*. In 1046 DR, he formally established the [[bakufu]] (shogunal military government) headquartered in [[Gifu]]. By relegating the [[Imperial Court]] to purely ceremonial and spiritual roles, Tademashi inaugurated nearly three centuries of [[Hojo]] military dominance over the realm.
