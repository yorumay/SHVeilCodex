A dedicated male tabaxi scholar and master artificer residing within the Wonderhome. Unlike the more aloof or eccentric inhabitants of the divine workshop, Tallec is the sole senior student who takes the party's arrival entirely seriously, acting as their primary tutor, guide, and grounding presence as they navigate the overwhelming scale of Gond's domain. Beneath his calm, feline demeanor and steady paws lies an encyclopedic knowledge of planar machinery, material stress points, and the eccentricities of both god and dragon.
