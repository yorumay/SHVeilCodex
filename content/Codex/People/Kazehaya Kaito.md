The enigmatic supreme leader of the Kazehaya ninja clan. A shadow figure whose real face and true past are unknown even to his most trusted lieutenants, Kaito operates through double-agents, illusion magic, and lightning-fast strikes. Under his ambitious leadership, the clan has moved beyond simple contract killings into grand cosmic play - orchestrating the theft of the Fan of Fujin for unknown patrons and steering the political instability of Kozakura from the darkness.
