The former [[Shogun]] of [[Kozakura]], son of Hojo Kikutake, and father to the current [[Shogun]], [[Hojo Kawabuko]]. Todahiro fought a brutal six-year conflict (The [[Hojo War]], 1341-1347 DR) to maintain his grip on the nation against rising noble coalitions. Following a catastrophic naval defeat off the island of [[Norinoshima]] in 1347 DR, he was stripped of power and banished to live in exile on the isolated island of [[Kanshu]], allowing his father in law, [[Takenaka Okawa]], to seize the government and place young [[Hojo Kawabuko|Kawabuko]] on the throne as a puppet. 

Years later, Todahiro raised a mercenary host on [[Kanshu]] and launched a desperate invasion to reclaim power - flaring bloody uprisings across [[Gizan]], [[Dosaki]], and Ike provinces - only for his forces to be ruthlessly crushed by the armies and [[ninja|shadow-assassins]] of his own son.
