The nominal [[shugo-daimyo]] governing the province of [[Tenmei]]. Though holding an official title, Motosune is widely known in secret to be a powerless figurehead. True authority over [[Tenmei]] has been usurped by fanatical advisors from the [[Temple of the Red Fog]] - a terrifying cult of fire-worshiping, human-sacrificing fanatics based in the volcanic depths of [[Daidake]]. Operating as a radical religious [[ikki|ikko-ikki]] movement, these zealots dicatate Motosune's every decree, transforming [[Tenmei]] into a dangerous haven for dark rituals and religious extremisms.
