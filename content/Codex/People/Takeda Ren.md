A master clay-artisan who became the tragic face of defiance in Kaisei. Following the Shogunate's secret invasion of the city, Councilor Hojo Sadamune brutally executed the city's garrison and defenders - a slaughter that claimed Ren's brother alongside his closest companions. As the sole surviving defender of that bloody purge, Ren stepped up to lead the underground rebellion against Hojo occupation forces, conducting hit-and-run sabotage until Shinzatsu arrived to strike down the tyrant.
