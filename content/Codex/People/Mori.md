The semi-mythical founder of the [[Akimatsu]] Dynasty and the first [[tenno|Emperor]] of [[Kozakura]]. As a human descendant of the last [[kunitsukami]] (Earth Spirit) ruler, Mori united the early warring tribes and ascended the throne in -73 DR - establishing the foundational year of the [[Kozakuran calendar]] and official recorded history. He originally etablished his court and seat of power in the coastal city of [[Fukama]] in [[Shizume]] Province.

Mori's life is enshrined in regional folklore and popular [[noh]] theatre, most famously in the classic drama "[[The Reward of Tomy]]." According to legend, the [[tenno|Emperor]] nearly perished after consuming an improperly prepared fugu (blowfish). He was saved by the quick thinking of a young apprentice chef named [[The Reward of Tomy|Sumitomo Tomy]]. In gratitude, Mori granted Tomy a stone inn located in [[Maeshi]] Province - the historic [[Doi no Fugu]] - which continues to operate and welcome travelers to this day.
