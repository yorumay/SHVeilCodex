A legendary korobokuru master blacksmith living in the secret mountain settlement of Kinzan. Gruff, short-tempered, and missing his right arm from past battles, Hagan's fiery disposition is matched only by his unrivaled mastery over tamahagane steel. Following the violent oni raids led by the fallen god Bokyo, Hagan took in the young survivor Kichi, passing down his lifelong forge wisdom and metalworking techniques to ensure the korobokuru craft survived the bloodshed.
