The formidable, long-reigning Shogun who broke the centuries-old cycle of puppet rulers. For generations, Hojo Shoguns were installed as child figureheads, only to be forced into retirement by powerful military regents (shikken) upon reaching adulthood. When the previous Shikken died and sparked a succession crisis, the nine-year-old Kawabuko acted with ruthless brilliance: he bought the loyalty of major ninja clans, orchestrated the assassination of every prospective regent, and seized absolute power for himself. Ruling directly for over thirty years through an era of covert assassinations known as the Shadow War, Kawabuko now faces the greatest threat to his reign with the rise of the Imperial Restoration.
