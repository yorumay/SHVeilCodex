An ancient, wise weasel-hengeyokai kami renowned for his uncanny foresight and celestial wisdom. His arrival on the peak that bears his name inspired the founding of the Hoshikage Monastery. When Akujin erupted from the earth two centuries ago, Izuna fought the fiery beast to protect his beloved wife, the yuki-onna Fuyukimi. Though Izuna struck the fatal blow and ripped out Akujin's Obsidian Heart, he was mortally wounded. With his final strength, he crawled to Fuyukimi's side - unaware that she had been tragically frozen by well-meaning monks - and died cradling her icy prison. His spirit lingered beside his bones for centuries until the party reunited the lovers, sparking his divine fusion into a new celestial deity.
