The brutal, bloodthirsty deity of murder, assassination, and ritualistic slaughter - and the third member of the infamous Dead Three alongside [[Bane]] and [[Myrkul]]. Unlike [[Bane]], who seeks to rule through terror, Bhaal delights solely in the act of killing itself. Foreseeing his own mortality during the [[Time of Troubles]], Bhaal walked [[Toril]] and fathered a multitude of mortal offspring - the Bhaalspawn - infusing each with a fragment of his divine essence in a sinister bid to ensure his eventual resurrection through blood and fratricide.
