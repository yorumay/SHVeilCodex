The Chaos Hound. Ravager of Heavens. An ancient, cosmic entity birthed directly from the primordial darkness of The Void. Driven by an absolute hatred for light, life, and divine order, this terrifying nightmare beast - resembling a massive, decaying hound dripping with acidic sludge and black flame - was awakened when creation first ignited. Myth tells that the Chaos Hound hunts divine souls across the cosmos leaving behind dead stars and silent skies in its wake.
