The supreme, unfathomable Overgod of Realmspace, standing infinitely above all pantheons, demon lords, and primordial entities. Ao neither seeks mortal worshipers nor grants spells to clerics; his sole cosmic mandate is maintaining the absolute balance of the crystal sphere and ensuring that the deities fulfill their designated duties. When the gods grew arrogant and neglected mortal prayers in pursuit of raw power, Ao shattered their complacency by casting them out of the heavens during Time of Troubles.
