Also known as **the Dragon Lords**, and the **Champions of the Jade Throne**

The core divine court standing directly beside the Jade Throne, ever ready to execute the divine will of the [[Celestial Emperor]]. Originally sent up the mortal world, these nine powerful deities govern every major facet of existence:
- **Ai Ch'ing**: Goddess of love, devotion, and holy marriage.
- **Chan Cheng**: God of bravery, martial arts, single combat, and war.
- **Ch'en Hsiang**: God of literature, music, poetry, and scholarship.
- **Chih Shih**: God of history, ancient lore, and sacred tradition.
- **Fa Kuan**: God of absolute justice, law, and retributive judgment.
- **Hsing Yong**: God of fortune, trade, and economic prosperity.
- **Kwan Ying**: Goddess of compassion, joy, and boudnless mercy.
- **Nung Chian**: God of agriculture, soil fertility, and bumper harvests.
- **Shu Chia**: God of artificers, craftsmanship, and the fine arts.
