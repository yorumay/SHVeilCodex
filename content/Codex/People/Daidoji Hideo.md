The patient administrator of Takeo, who has governed the village under an unresolved bureaucratic mistake for over twenty years. Following the death of his father, Daidoji Razan, the young Hideo was meant to yield the estate to a new retired veteran and relocate his family. When no successor arrived, Hideo stepped into the breach - faithfully managing the town's affairs, maintaining order, and meticulously paying Takeo's annual taxes to the province. Despite submitting numerous petitions over two decades requesting a formal replacement, Hideo remains in administrative limbo, serving as Takeo's dutiful lord while waiting for an answer that may never come.
