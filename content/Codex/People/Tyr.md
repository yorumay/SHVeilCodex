![[Tyr-1786607797686.webp|256]]A major deity of the Faerûnian pantheon who embodies law, justice, duty, and righteous warfare. Depicted as a stern, one-handed warrior with a blindfold or scarred eyes, Tyr demands absolute adherence to oaths, moral integrity, and the defense of the innocent. In Kozakura, foreign paladins and high-minded samurai who revere Tyr view his philosophy as the purest divine manifestation of giri (duty) and strict moral law.
