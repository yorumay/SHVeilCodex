A destructive primordial elemental of pure fire whose violent emergence onto the Material Plane two centuries ago triggered a cataclysmic eruption of Izunayama. Though slain in that ancient conflict by the god Izuna, Akujin left behind his Obsidian Heart - a burning relic that continued to pulse with fiery malice. Two hundred years later, a corrupted cult within the Hoshikage monastery used bio-magical engineering to harness the Heart, intending to manifest Akujin once more. When Abbot Gen'un attempted the final ritual, the Heart consumed him, drawing Akujin back through a planar tear. The elemental was permanently destroyed by Shinzatsu before he could reclaim his Heart, extinguishing his infernal threat forever.
