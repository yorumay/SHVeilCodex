Cunning, monstrous humanoids possessing the innate magical ability to perfectly mimic the physical form, voice, and minor mannerisms of any creature they observe. Operating as master manipulators, spies, and covert assassins, doppelgangers murder key figures and seamlessly steal their identitites to infiltrate noble houses, guilds, and military command structures. Because they leave no physical footprint of their true form and can read the surface thoughts of their victims, a single doppelganger can hollow out an entire council or adventuring party from within before anyone realizes a threat exists.
