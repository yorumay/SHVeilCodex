An aristocratic or powerful spirit transformed into a malevolent phantom by dying in a state of profound betrayal, rage, or injustice. Unlike lesser ghosts, a goryo possess formidable spiritual influence - capable of summoning devastating blights, sudden storms, plague outbreaks, or unnatural fires to torment those who wronged them (or their descendants). Only elaborate exorcisms, imperial pardons, or grand public shrines erected to appease their wrath can pacify a restless goryu.
