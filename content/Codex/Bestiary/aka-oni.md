The most common and violently hot-tempered subrace of true oni, instantly recognizable by their fiery crimson skin, dual iron horns, and wild, unkempt hair. Driven by unbridled rage, pride, and an insatiable appetite for raw flesh, aka-oni serve as frontline champions, vanguard commanders, and terrifying enforcers for dark warlords. Wielding massive spiked iron clubs ([[kanabo]]) and hurling fire-infused curses, an aka-oni relies on brute force and overwhelming intimidation to crush any who stand in its path.
