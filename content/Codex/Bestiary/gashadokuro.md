A colossal skeleton formed from the combined malice, despair, and unburied bones of hundreds of soldiers who died of starvation or perished on blood-soaked battlefields. Roaming deep forests and abandoned war zones at night, a gashadokuro makes no sound except the quiet rattling of its massive bones - until it sneaks up on unsuspecting travelers, bites off their heads, and drinks their spilled blood to feed its eternal, unquenchable hunger.
