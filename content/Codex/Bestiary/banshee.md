A terrifying, incorporeal undead spirit born from the corrupted soul of a female elf or spirit-touched mortal who used her beauty and grace to betray others. Cursed in death to reflect the grotesque malice of her true nature, a banshee lingers in desolate ruins, dark mountain passes, and abandoned battlefields.

Driven by an insatiable hatred for the living, her presence is heralded by a bitter chill and a soul-shattering mournful wail. Hearkening closely to the vengeful [[onryo]] and sorrowful [[yurei]] of Kozakuran folklore, a banshee's lament is fatal to those who hear it, capable of instantly dropping hardened warriors to the brink of death and shattering the resolve of entire adventuring parties.
