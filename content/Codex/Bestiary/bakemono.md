A broad, umbrella term for weird monsters, mischievous tricksters, and monstrous beasts capable of shifting their form. Unlike noble kami or tragic ghosts, bakemono are volatile, unpreditable creatures - ranging from goblin-like earth spirits to shapeshinfting animals (tanuki, kitsune, badger spirits) that trick foolish travelers, hoard stolen treasures, or infiltrate human settlements disguised as monks, beautiful maidens, or traveling merchants.
