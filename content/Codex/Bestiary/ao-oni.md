A lethal, aristocratic subrace of pureblood oni recognizable by their deep indigo or sapphire skin, single curved horn, and cold, calculating intellect. Unlike their red-skinned, rage-filled kin ([[aka-oni]]), the ao-oni are master strategists, sorcerers, and ice-or-water manipulators. They prefer to rule behind the scenes - corrupting local warlords, orchestrating political assassinations, and commanding armies of lesser monsters with terrifying, dispassionate efficiency.
