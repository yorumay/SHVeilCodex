A terrifying, violent class of yurei consumed entirely by pure, unadulterated vengeance. Born when a mortal suffers an agonizingly unjust death, an onryo's hatred is so absolute that it tears through the veil of death, granting the spirit formidable supernatural power over the physical world. Capable of altering local weather, inflicting deadly curses (tatari), manifesting horrifying hallucinations, and murdering their tormentors - along with anyone unluckily caught in their path - an onryo cannot be permanently laid to rest through brute force alone. True peace only comes when absolute justice is delivered or their profound grudge (unedachi) is resolved.
