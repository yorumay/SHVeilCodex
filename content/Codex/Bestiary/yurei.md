A broad classification for the lingering spirits of mortals who died in the grip of intense emotion - grief, betrayal, sudden trauma, or unfulfilled duty. Bound to the mortal realm by the denial of a peaceful passage to Yomi, a yurei typically appears as a floating, tranlucent figure clad in white burial robes (kataginu or shiro-shozoku), with limp hands, disheveled hair, and no visible feet. While some yurei are merely sorrowful haunts seeking proper burial rites or the fulfillment of a final wish, ignoring their grievances risks allowing their sorrow to curdle into deadly malice.
