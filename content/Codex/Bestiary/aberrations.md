Unnatural, horrific entities that possess no rightful place within the natural order of the [[Nakatsukuni|Prime Material Plane]], the elemental domains, or the recognized planar cosmology. It is widely understood by planar sorcerers and [[shukenja]] that aberrations originate from - or have been deeply corrupted by - the terrifying, madness-inducing expanse known as the [[Far Realm]].

Because they are governed by non-Euclidian geometry and planar laws completely alien to local reality, aberrations exhibit bizarre physical anatomies, psionic compulsions, and motives that baffle mortal comprehension. From tentacled mind-eaters and eye-covered horrors to writing masses of flesh, their mere presence tears at the fabric of sanity and warps the surrounding environment into grotesque, unnatural distortions.
