title: 3. Wrath of the Mountains
---
![[index-1786008999564.webp]]

The winter that came early, and the monastery at the eye of it. The lord's envoys had gone north to Hoshikage after a prophecy delivered by a monk who died on his floor, adn had not come back. The six went after them through a storm that was killing the province, and found the house besieged by the dead, and a new faith of fire being kept in its cellars over the bones of the old one. What ended it was not the blade. Beneath the mountain a woman had been sealed in ice for a hundred years, and grief had made a storm of her, and it was words that let her put it down at last. They came out of that mountain having watched a kami made.
