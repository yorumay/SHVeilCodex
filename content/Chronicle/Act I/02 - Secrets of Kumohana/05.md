## Of the Company of the Spider, and of the Empty Shrine

The trail north went through woods that did not want them. Spiders came down out of the canopy upon silk that did not look strange so near to [[Kumohana]]'s doorposts, and these they cut down and went on. The cave was lit and it was guarded and they went into it without ceremony. None of the dead had names that morning. The names came afterward, out of journals taken from the deeper rooms.

Kumo no Maru they had called themselves, the company of the spider. They had been soldiers, abandoned by superiors who did not set their own names to the abandoning, and one Kuro had led them south, having judged that if the empire would not keep him he would carve out a lesser empire of his own. The sigil they took because [[Kumohana]] already wore it upon every lintel, and it is easier to bend a thing that is feared than to build one. Kuro was dead by then, and the rest with him.

They came back under a low moon and found the town's hurt greater than they had reckoned; the elder's daughter lay wounded at the shrine in the [[miko|shrine maiden]]'s care, and the young [[ronin]] took the watch at the gate. They slept, believing the work finished. The morning came wrong. The shrine was empty and the [[ronin]] stood in the doorway with his sword drawn and his eyes the wrong colour, moving as a man moves who is defending his beloved, and what he defended was an empty room. They broke the charm before the charm broke him, and he could not say where the night had gone. Within, the place had been left in haste — bandages dark with what they had carried, offering bowls untouched, blood upon the threshold and a path of it going into the trees. The two women had gone into the forest before dawn. Which of them walked and which was carried, the record does not say.
