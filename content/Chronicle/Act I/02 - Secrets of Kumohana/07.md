## Of the Spider of Kumohana, and of What the Village Chose

The main hall held what two hundred years of [[Kumohana]] had preferred not to know. Bodies. Bandits the village had never recovered, [[ronin]] no master had come seeking, vagabonds and travellers and the small currency of persons who pass through a place and are forgotten. She had been thorough, and she had also been patient. They found her in the deepest shrine, seated in perfect [[seiza]] with her back to the door, and upon the altar before her was the head of the elder's daughter, set down with the care that flowers are set down. _You should not have come,_ she said, and did not turn. _This is a place of worship, and not for the living._ Then she stood, and turned, and ceased to be a woman. Her arms drew long and her fingers became blades; her spine bent in a direction spines do not bend; her legs unmade themselves and were remade backward, and beneath her the great body rose pulsing upon eight clicking points, and her face split open in a smile that would not close. _You will make fine offerings for the kami,_ she said, and came at them.

The fight was long and twice it nearly went the other way. At the last she tried to flee, going up across the high beams toward some crack in the temple's bones she had used before, and they pulled her down. She died upon the floor of the hall she had held for three hundred years.

They carried the news back to [[Kumohana]] and the news was not received as they had thought it would be. The lilies in the village beds had turned to ash in the night. The granaries did not bless the rice. The wells ran thin. Whatever she had given the village in exchange for what she took, she had been giving it a long while. They heard the word [[jorogumo]], and looked at the empty fields, and looked at the strangers who had brought the demon's death into their houses, and made the reckoning in their own minds, and chose. The six left under no one's blessing. South at the lord's hall the work was named done, and a second matter closed there that they had not known they carried: the company of the spider had been the same men the young [[ronin]] had sworn to find, who had killed his master. The vow ended in a cave he never entered, by hands that were not his. The lord named him [[samurai]] of the [[Honda]] for an answer he had not been given the chance to write. The [[geisha and maiko|maiko]] went with him into the court. Eight had ridden out of [[Fukama]]. Six rode on.
