The festival of the first snow at Kumohana, and what it cost the village to be rid of what it was living on. The six went as their lord's witnesses, drove off bandits who had been marked already by another hand, and were followed home by the consequence: the shrine maiden of that place was a jorogumo, and had been feeding upon travellers for three centuries, and the white lilies that were the village's whole prosperity were her gift. They killed her in the old temple above the town. The lilies turned to ash before morning. Kumohana did not thank them.
