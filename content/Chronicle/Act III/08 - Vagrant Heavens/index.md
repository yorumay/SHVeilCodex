The climb up Seiryuuyama and what was said to them at the top of it. The mountain was three days of things that had been waiting on that road since the pilgrims stopped coming - beasts out of an age not their own, gnolls in a rock that had been holding gnolls for a lifetime, driders, and at the last a dragon out of the higher cloud with the blue fire flickering between its scales. What the summit altar required of them was neither blood nor treasure nor any deed, but truth, and each of them gave it. Beyond the door a god received them and named the work: The Ravager of Heavens is loose, and must be bound again. Three bound it at the dawn of the world and three must bind it now, and Fujin told them where those three had gone. He told them one thing more, which concerned only one of them, and had been true since before that one was born.
