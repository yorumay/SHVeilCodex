---
title: 6. Night of Bluefire
---
![[index-1786009013075.webp]]

The eight hours that changed Fukama, and Kozakura, and Kara-Tur, and the whole of Toril, and a good deal beyond it. It began as an ordinary day of spring and there is nothing in the morning of it worth keeping. By nightfall the sky over the city had cracked, and behind the crack there was nothing at all, and out of the nothing came a fire that was blue and burned what fire cannot burn. What the six did that night they did with strength and with cunning and with no understanding whatever of what they were standing in. They cut a road across a city that had stopped being a city they knew and reached the old Imperial Castle, which is the ancestral seat of the Akimatsu, and held there against the dead and against things that had no names in this country and at the last against the lord of fire himself. Fukama was standing in the morning.
