## Of the Three Tests Upon the South-Western Road

The road out of [[Fukama]] bent south-west and set three tests upon them, and not one of the three was of the kind that draws steel. At the second mile a [[Bakufu]] patrol stopped them and accepted papers that were not altogether papers. At Suzume-no-yado men of the [[Bakufu]] had come to take the [[Honda]] who sheltered there, and it was the [[wu jen]] [[Kenji]] who counselled withdrawal over arrest, and his enforcers withdrew at his word. _Even shogunate forces value life over death,_ he said; and the six carried that sentence with them a long way afterward, and it is set down here because they did.

At the [[Honda]] waystation a peddler laid out curiosities before them, and they looked past the seeming and saw the [[tanuki]] beneath it, and his frightened kin arranged upon the cloth as merchandise. They let them go. Past the shrine of [[Okuninushi]] and a cold night at a stranger's fire they crossed the hills to the coast and turned east, and at the city's edge a woman was waiting who had been sent to wait for them. The resistance of [[Kaisei]] laid its plans before them and they pledged what they had to give, and before that night was out the aqueducts beneath the city were opening to let them in.
