The road south-west out of a broken city, and the killing at the end of it. The six went out of Fukama three days after the Sending with a lord's writ and a god's errand, and the country they crossed was not the country the maps had promised them. Three tests met them on the road and none of the three was settled with steel. What waited at Kaisei was a city under occupation and a Roju sitting in another man's hall, who received them with tea and courtesy and a lie about a dragon, and gave them a pearl, and called them idiots for not asking more of him. He was correct about very little else. That night the druid stayed behind when the others left, and went where a small lizard may go, and Hojo Sadamune died in his own bath without ever learning who had come for him.
