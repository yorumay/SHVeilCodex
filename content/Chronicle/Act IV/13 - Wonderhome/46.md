## Record of the Dwarven Halls at Wonderhome, and of the Thievery Within Them

They spent two days learning the halls the [[Gond|god]] had given them. The stone palace was made by dwarven lords for their own use, and later enlarged for the bastion of an adventuring guild, and the [[Shinzatsu|six]] found traces of both in its rooms and passages. On the second day a [[Saito Junichi|guest]] came who had not been asked. He had killed several of the students who had helped the six, and he had taken the [[Gondar armour]] from its place. He put it on in secret and tried to walk out, and the metal's own properties hardened him against their magic. The [[Shinzatsu|six]] surrounded him and closed the way out. They broke the armour from his body, and he left them by a spell that the god's enemies had given him.

[[Gond]] came back to the palace, and found the aftermath. He praised the six, and was plainly impressed by what they had done against the armour he had made for the [[Inquisitor]]s of [[Cyric]]. He then told them how the multiverse stood. Planes drifted like leaves upon the sea. Many gods were gone or worse. The [[Elder Eternal Evils|Elder Evils]] of [[Dendar]] and [[Ityak-Ortheel]] were still in their prisons, and [[Kezef]] was loose, has had been feared. The six asked to be returned to the [[nakatsukuni|Prime Material]], to [[Daidake]], where Yondai's family was still held by the [[Temple of the Red Fog|Red Fog]]. Gond agreed, and sent them with a sending stone bound to Ordalane.

The [[Shinzatsu|six]] came out upon the peaks of [[Daidake]], and below them the burning landscape spread out, the town of [[Akayu]] among it, and the elementals raged upon the lava that pooled across the dying mountain.
