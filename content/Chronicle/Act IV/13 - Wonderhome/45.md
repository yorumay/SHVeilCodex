## Record of the Gate Closed Beneath the Lava, and of the God Who Came Late

At the end of the cave beyond the river of fire stood a statue that no one had carved to any name. There the world was open, and [[aberrations|things]] were crawling through the opening, and the six went against them. They laid salt in a ring about the wound, and spoke the words of meaning, and it shut.

Then the [[Gond|god]] came, with his constructs about him, and he was alte. The [[Ordalane|copper dragon]] who had been watching remarked upon it. He answered that someone had changed the password. He was quick in mind and in speech, and in the same breath he understood what had been done: the six had worked planar magic upon his realm with kitchen wares, and had succeeded where he and the dragon had failed for three months. He named them [[Shinzatsu|six of the prophecy]]. He gave them room and food and rest, and told them of the [[Elder Eternal Evils|Eternal Evils]] of the first dawn, and of the [[Kezef|Hound]]. He charged them to seek the other two of the three who bound it before, and every god who could be persuaded to join the binding once more. 

The six were left with a choice of roads: to stay and train in the [[Wonderhome|forge]], to return to [[Daidake]] and seek a family there, to labour in the god's cellars over a captured sun, or to go out after the gods. The god himself went up to the [[Mount Celestia|high mountain]] to learn what the gods knew.
