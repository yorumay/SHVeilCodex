## Record of the Road to Toturi, and of the Nave at Akayu

They were debriefed aboard the admiral's flagship and paid for the [[Suzaku]] with a tartane, a ship of their own to cross the straits. They took the twenty-four over in it. Two days and a little more on the water brought them ashore a few miles short of [[Yoshida]], where they drew the vessel up onto the beach and hid it, and went inland on foot. A day and a half they climbed through mountains, and what lived in them kept its distance. They came to [[Toturi]] by nightfall. The villagers scraped together a feast from what they had, and over it told of the [[stonewatcher]], and of the dark in the mine, and of [[Ishikui Yondai|Yondai]]. In the morning the six left them and went toward [[Akayu]].

Their first stop was Yuwatari, a shrine in a forested valley, and they were expected there. They were received as heroes and as champions, and were escorted up the mountain to [[Akayu]], where the [[Temple of the Red Fog]] keeps its seat. The road passed without incident. At [[Akayu]], the keeper of the tavern gave them his own room, tand they slept in it.

In the morning they met [[Hanomoto Genji]] in the nave of the Temple. They sat in the pews. He spoke slowly, and gave thanks and they asked him a few questions, and the talk ended in violence. [[Saito Junichi]] struck off the old man's head and threw a key to the six, and said only: "He was going to make a speech. It was about to be boring. You should check under his villa, he's been keeping secrets from his followers."
