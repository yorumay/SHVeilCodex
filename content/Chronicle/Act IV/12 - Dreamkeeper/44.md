## Record of the Sleeping Woman, and of the Forge Beyond the Whirlpool

They broke the [[xorn]] and opened the door, and a second working loosened the lock that held it. Beyond lay a stair and an underground river that now ran with magma. At its foot stood a statue of [[Hellenism|Charon]], and before if a pool of coins, laid there for the fare of the departed. Above, in the sleeper's chamber, three curved pillars of jade crackled with mist between them, and they fell asleep before they could turn away.

In that sleep they stood upon a stone platform in a void. A face rose from a ring of stones before them, the face of the [[Hanomoto Yomogi|sleeper]], with eyes like pools of dark. From its mouth a [[Izanami|hand]] came up, long in the nail, and from the hand a [[skittering horror]] came forth and fell upon them. They broke it. As it died the hand turned within the mouth and drove its nails into the face, and with a shriek they woke in the catacomb. It was withered now, and bloody, and abandoned. The [[Hanomoto Yomogi|sleeper]] had mummified into what she ought to have been, and they could not be sure the nightmare had ended until its weight had lifted from them.

They took the amulet from the dead woman and opened the gate at the river's end, where the stream now ran red where it had run with fire. A ferry lay at the pier, and they rowed down the whirlpool and came out in a field before a mountain. There were artificers there who told them where they were: [[Wonderhome]], the forge of [[Gond]]. A copper dragon named [[Ordalane]], whom the students called the only friend of the god, asked them a few quick questions and then left in haste for an appointment. Her hoard held the works of every student who had every graduated from that place. She asked whether they knew of holes in the floor, from which ugly things poured out, and said she and the god had been fighting them. The [[Shinzatsu|six]] were left to plan a crossing of the lava toward the hole, while the dragon observed them.
