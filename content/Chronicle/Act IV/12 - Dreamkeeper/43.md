## Record of the Escape from the Temple, and of the Tomb Beneath the Villa

They fought their way out of the [[Temple of the Red Fog|Temple]] and got clear before the reinforcements could close upon them. They did not take the road. They circled the town and entered the villa from the river, and a short search of its rooms showed them what lay behind the daughter of [[Hanomoto Genji]], and the reverence that is paid to her this day.

Beneath the villa were the catacombs, and in them they learned of a hidden faith: an [[yomi-no-kuni|underworld]] that was not of this country, whose power the priests of the [[Temple of the Red Fog|Temple]] had learned to tap. At the last they found the daughter. She lay dormant, her body alive and her mind caught in nightmares. She pleaded with them, and her plea was of her father, who had kept her alive. They did not act on it.

Beyond her they found a statue of [[Hellenism|Hades]]. A trap had been set about it, and they sprang it, and six [[xorn]] came out of the stone.
