The Red Fog had been worshiping the god of fire and in its place had found the gods of the underworld. Imbibing on the white flower that grew in the caves in the mountains the priests were able to visit the underworld, learn of its many gods and customs.

Eventually, the daughter of the highpriest Hanomoto Genji fell into eternal sleep. The cult thought she had died, while Genji kept her hidden and alive in the catacombs. Her mind becoming a beacon for the darkness beneath, and tormenting the dreams of all above.

Shinzatsu came, and put an end to her nightmare, ending the reign of the Dreamkeeper.
