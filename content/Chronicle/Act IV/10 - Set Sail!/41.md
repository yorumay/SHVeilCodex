## Record of the Cost of the Crossing at Okahina, and of the Burning of Suzaku

They came into [[Okahina]] in the middle of the night in a thunderstorm, and the town began robbing them before they had found a dry doorway. They learned quickly what kind of place it was. Some coin was saved by quick thinking - the [[thri-kreen]] was declared an exotic pet - and in the morning, having registered him with the port as such, they asked the price of a crossing to [[Yoshida]] and were told seven thousand gold a head. They asked about the ship called [[Suzaku]], which had paid nothing to that harbour in six years, and were sent to the admiral [[Kirishima Daito|Daito]]. They put the bargain to him before he could put it to them: deal with [[Suzaku]], and be carried across the water for it. He agreed, and gave them what help he could.

They went abroad from the stern, straight into the captain's quarters, and tried words. The words failed at once. The alarm went the length of the ship, and the crew came at them, and the crew's mage threw fire that caught in the timbers and did not stop. What followed was fought on a deck that was burning and in a hold that was filling with smoke. Most of the crew died. Fifteen of the people chained below died also, killed by the crew as the fight turned, so that there should be no one left to say what the Suzaku had been carrying.

The six got the rest of them out. Thirty-nine had been taken; twenty-four came off that ship alive, into the water and into boats. [[Kirishima Daito|Daito]]'s people saw the [[Suzaku]] burning from the harbour and sent a vessel for survivors.
