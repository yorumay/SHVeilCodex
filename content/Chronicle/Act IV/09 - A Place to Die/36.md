## Of the Prison at Kaisei, and of the Man Who Did Not Need Rescuing

[[Kaisei]] had reclaimed itself while they were upon the mountain. The [[Bakufu]] forces were penned in the fortress, besieged and leaderless; [[Hojo Sadamune|Sadamune]]'s death had not yet been spoken aloud in the city, but the shape of it was visible in every order that was not given and every officer waiting upon instruction that would not come. [[Takeda Ren]] met them with the quiet satisfaction of a man whose long fight has at last gone the right way, and then, because the work is never only the work, he told them of the prisoner. [[Saito Junichi]] had been taken a week before, claiming their name as his own.

They went to the prison to put questions to him, and found instead a prison in the middle of an eruption. [[Intellect devourer]]s wearing the bodies of fallen guards. [[Mezzoloth]]s working sorcery from the ends of the corridors. [[Allip]]s drifting through walls never built to stop such things. It was a rescue, coordinated and expensive, mounted for a man who had not asked for one and, as became apparent, had no need of one. A [[cacophonous chimera]] waited in the yard and kept its voice upon them throughout, which is a psychic noise, and sharp, and binds the limbs. More than once the fight came near the edge. It came back from the edge because they made it come back.

[[Saito Junichi]] walked out of his cell when the dust had settled. He found [[Shinchuu]], and the duel was not long. He won it, with the chimera's voice still in the air and the [[aberrations]] still dying on the stones, and the blow he dealt was not a killing one. Then he mounted the beast in a cloud of ink and it rose, and he looked down and said: _I'll finish what you started._ The line is recorded without interpretation, none having been offered and none being available. The chimera took him east. The prison went quiet. The [[thri-kreen]] was raised, and lived.
