## Record of the Trouble at Takeo, and of the Tomb Upon the Ridge

[[Takeo]] gave them its trouble in the order a village gives trouble: the headman first, then the village elder, then the healer woman. Cattle gone off the high pasture. People gone from the road. Three bodies recovered in the weeks before, and the state of those bodies is the part this account sets down carefully, because the state of them was the whole of what the village knew. The skulls had been taken. The flesh had been taken. What remained of the bone had been crushed - not gnawed, and not broken by any fall, but crushed.

The old story came out. Every village keeps some version of it. An [[omukade]] that wrapped the mountain seven times about and coiled the remained of its length down into the lake below, feeding upon whoever came to the water; and a hero, and an arrow, and the saliva upon the arrowhead. The centipede died. The village endured. The story is old enough now to be told as a story and not as a warning. The heard it out, and thought upon crushed bone and upon how much of a thing one would need for seven coils, and said nothing for a moment. 

Next morning they left, for the tomb upon the ridge. The Tomb of Anrakuson opened to them without violence, which was the last courtesy it extended. There were pillars, and symbols upon them, and a sequence to be read and repeated in order; they stood before it some while, and then the door. No blood was spent upon the threshold. Within, the place took back what it had given. The first corridor dropped half of them into a pit and dead came down after them, and they climbed, and fought, and cleared the chamber - and then opened the sarcophagi in the chamber beyond it, which woke everything the first fight had left sleeping. [[Hisakawa Fuurin]] went ahead seeking cover and found a crushing-wall trap instead, and survived it, and was immediately surrounded by four of the dead. The trap reset itself. They filled the gap between its walls. One of the wights, having evidently arrived at an idea, threw itself upon the trigger-plate to bring the walls down upon the four of them at once. It did not work. The attempt is recorded here regardless, being the most interesting thing the dead did that evening. They took a short breath in the cleared rooms. The tomb went on ahead of them.
