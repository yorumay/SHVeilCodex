## Record of the Guardians at the Door, and of the Fire That Was Lit

![[39-1787032241643.webp]]

The hands of [[Anrakuson]] stood between them and the tomb proper. They studied the statue, and considered whether it was mending it wanted, and whether some courtesy was owed to it - and then turned instead to the working of the door. So the guardians woke. What followed was longer and worse than any of them had reckoned for: the great golem laying its slowness upon them again and again, and the lesser things about it keeping it from harm. When it was finished the door was open. 

Below, they found [[banshee]]s, and did the thing that is not often done: they spoke to them, and asked, and waited. The dead gave them nothing useful. Then one of them broke, and what came out of her before her wail took the air was this - *Anrakuson lied.* It is written here exactly as it was given, because in every telling that survives it is given exactly so, and no teller has ever agreed on what was meant by it. They set food before the inner door and went down.

![[39-1787032250598.webp]]

In the grand tomb they were careful. They read the sarcophagi and did not open them; they weighed what the priest's journal had said against the seven pillars and could not make the two agree. And then [[Hisakaawa Fuurin|Fuurin]] set torches burning around the chamber, for light, and for nothing else. But the welcoming fire for the returning spirits had never been lit in that place, not once in sixty days, and the tomb took the lighting for the rite it had been waiting on. The [[omukade]] came out. It very nearly finished them; it took [[Saejima Ken|Ken-ha]] whole in his tiger's shape and had begun on the rest before [[Shinchuu]] put an end to it. Afterward, set into one of the plates of its armoured length, they found a crest, and the crest was the mon of the [[Daidoji]]. [[Okamura Renjishi|Guroutsu]] cut it free and carried it away with him. Then they went down again, into the abandoned levels, and saw the work had been begun there and left unfinished.
