## Record of the Fifty-Two in the Abandoned Tomb, and of What Was Sealed Beneath It

![[40-1787387111951.webp]]

They didived to search the abandoned tomb, and so woke what lay in it. Fifty-two came out of the coffins there, and there was no way back past them, and so they found a manner of standing and fighting that worked and killed the whole fifty-two. There were others in that place also, warriors woken by Anrakuson and armed and ready to stand with them, and in the end they were not needed. When it was done they spoke - *do not go down* - and went back into their coffins to wait until they should be wanted again.

![[40-1787387116482.webp]]

On the lowest floor was the blood altar. Twelve bodies laid in a circle. A circle painted about them and broken in one place. And a foreign body among the rest, whose belongings said plainly enough where he had come from and what he had come to do: that Shou Lung had learned the priest of Takeo was slack in his duties, and had sent a man to make use of it. Ken-ha, going deeper still, found the chasm beneath, and the rift open in the bottom of it, and called them to it.

Then the sound came off the walls - *gachi,gachi* - and the gashadokuro came up. They had salt, and they had the words. They threw the ring and it reached over the ring and struck them; they threw again and oit broke a storm of bone across the chamber that very nearly finished Shinchuu where he stood; they threw a third time and it turned up the last man standing near it, which was Yondai. It was not enough. The last of the words were said and the rift was sealed and the thing that had been climbing was dragged down. Afterward they looked at the twelve adn could not tell one from another, so gave them all the same rite, and burned them, and said the words that should have been said for them at last.  So the dead of Takeo were let go at last.

They climbed out and told Daidoji Hideo what had been beneath his village - a skeleton made of other skeletons, which he named for them - and of the foreign hand that had made it, which he guessed correctly before they finished telling him. *Why us?* he said. He had no answer and they had none to give him. He paid them what he could - food and lodging - and told them what he had heard of the roads ahead, and they went on toward Okahina.
