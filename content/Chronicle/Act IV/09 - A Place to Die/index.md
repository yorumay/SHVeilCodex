A short detour, and very nearly the last one. Takeo is a place where distinguished men go to die well, and something beneath it had been eating the people who lived there. The six went in expecting a yokai and found instead that a rift to Yomi had been opened in the ancestral tomb of the settlement, and had been standing open since the night the sky broke, and that what came up out of it had been climbing sixty days. They closed it. They were fortunate to close it. Twelve men were taken down into that mountain as individuals and come back up as one thing, and could not be told apart afterward, and were burned together and given the rites that had been owed them at the first. The countryside stopped losing people. Nobody in Takeo was ever told the whole of it, including the man who sent them down.
