title: 4. Fourteen Nights
---

The theft of the Fan of Fujin, and the fortnight given to answer for it. The Tengu of Ak'rat came down to Fukama for the Festival of Divine Descent and brought their dynastic relic with them, and it was taken in the night by thieves wearing the faces of the six. What saved them was an onmyoza of the crane line who read the Weave and showed the chamber that the faces had been worn and not owned. They were given fourteen nights to bring back the relic and their lord's honour with it. They chased the Kazehaya east through swamp and village and an under-town beneath Hakuba, and came to the fam at last in ruins whose name no one could read - and lost it there, taken up out of their hands by the wind itself, which had come for its own. The Tengu had been following them the whole way and judging the work by what they saw of it. That judgement was the thing they actually won.
