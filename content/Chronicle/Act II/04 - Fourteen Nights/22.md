## Of the Recovery of the Fan, and of the Deeper Ruin

Wave came after wave, and then the [[tengu]] came. [[Shi'rokha]]'s people had followed since the road, watching the chase from a distance and judging the work by what they saw of it, and when the higher men of the [[Kazehaya]] pressed hardest the [[tengu]] fell upon them out of the high places of the ruin and broke them. _Their actions have spoken,_ the [[tengu]] said, and so withdrew the accusation, and invited them to [[Ak'rat]] when the time should suit. The fan hung a hundred feet up in a sphere of stilled air that no working could touch from without. They solved it sideways: they laid what spells they had upon [[Shinchuu]] and threw the [[thri-kreen]] skyward like a cast blade, and at the height of his arc he reached the sphere and broke it. The fan went free, and the wind in it went free, and the stalker that had been bound up beside it went free also. He came down hard. The fan went up faster, borne away upon the stalker's keeping, and the [[tengu]] went after it on wings. So the matter that began in [[Fukama]] with the breaking open of a chamber door ended upon a hillside in the copper hills, with the relic returned to the pursuit of those it belonged to. Their part in it was done.

Then the ruin finished opening. Beneath the binding-circle was a deeper place, older than anything they had a frame for — pale veined marble with a blue light still living in the veins of it, a structure tilted steeply upon some unseen edge, and life clinging to walls that had not been built with that gravity in mind. A druid stood at a font of pale fire in the heart of the chamber with a great working open between his hands and dark things closing on him from every side, and his beast held the line, and he called for help. They joined it. [[Saejima Ken|Ken-ha]] he named himself when there was breath for it, apprentice to a teacher they had met in passing the week before, sent there to seal a crack in the veil between worlds. What lay on the other side of that crack had been bleeding through the place a long while, and this account will say only that it was a world of darkness and of despair, and leave it there.

The font gave them a vision they had not asked for while they fought: a throne room of white marble and gold canted at a violent angle, and a man in robes too rich for him gripping a broken balcony rail as the world he stood in fell, screaming. _He tried to take [[the weave]] itself! He tried to steal her power! [[Karsus]] has doomed us all!_ The name is written here because it was heard. The hall fell and the vision went dark. Afterward they went deeper into the ruin than the druid wished them to go, and what was in the deep part of it is not set down. They came back at a run. The halfling came back hurt in a way the others were not, carrying his sword-arm cradled against his chest, and he did not show it to them. The druid closed the working and the font went out and the ruin came down behind them, and seven stood on the road when the dust cleared, and none of them spoke much for some hours. At the fire the druid asked whether he might travel with them and they said that he might. Late, when the others were down, the halfling rose to go, and found the [[thri-kreen]] already waiting at the bend in the road. What passed between them is not written. He would not carry what he now carried toward people who had not asked for it. The [[thri-kreen]] let him go, and did not promise to follow him, and promised instead to find a cure.
