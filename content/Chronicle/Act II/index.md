The shortest of the parts, and the one that changes everything that comes after it.

Two tellings only. In the first the six are accused of a theft they did not commit and are given a fortnight to prove it, and they fail to recover what was taken and are judged worthy anyway - not by the court that accused by them, but by those who followed behind them the whole way and watched how they went about it. In the second they are carried up above the cloud and told what the fortnight was for.

What is given to them there is not a task so much as a position. The realms above are fractured and cannot reach down; the courts beside are fractured and cannot reach across; and so the work that must be done can only be done by hands that are already standing on the ground. They are named Emissaries of the Middle Realm, which is a title n oone had held before and no one has held since. From this part onward the six are not adventurers taking a lord's coin. They are the instrument of powers who cannot act for themselves, which is a different thing to be, and a worse one.
