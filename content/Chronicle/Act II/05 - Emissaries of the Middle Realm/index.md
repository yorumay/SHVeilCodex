title: 5. Emissaries of the Middle Realm
---
![[index-1786009008727.webp]]

The city above the cloud, and what was asked of them in it. The Tengu carried the six up to Ak'rat and they waited at the top of a tree a mile high until it suited two goddesses to come through the frame at its summit. What was said there was that the divine realms were fractured and could not act, and that the fey courts were fractured and could not act, and that mortal hands were the only hands left that could touch the work. They were named Emissaries of the Middle Realm and sent to Seiryuuyama to stand before Fujin. More was said than has been written. They came down of the tree and took the road home to Fukama, and the account of this part ends there, because what happened at Fukama belongs to a different telling.
